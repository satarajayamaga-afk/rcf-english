    How to use this site
    Five stages

    Our learning approach

    Every lesson on RCF English follows the same progression. It is deliberately gradual, because most learners fail at writing not through lack of ability but through lack of preparation.

    1. Understand

      Receive a clear explanation of the lesson or the task, in plain language.

    2. Observe

      Study a useful model and see exactly how it is put together.

    3. Practise

      Complete activities with guidance, one step at a time.

    4. Apply

      Use what you have learnt independently, without the model in front of you.

    5. Review

      Find your own mistakes with a checklist, correct them, and strengthen what you know.

    The platform

    About RCF English

    RCF English is a comprehensive educational platform created to bring together English-language learning, literature education, examination support, teacher resources, online classes and educational publications in one accessible place.

    It serves school students, O/L and A/L learners, adult learners, English teachers, tuition teachers and parents. Particular attention is given to learners who require additional guidance, including those in rural and remote areas with limited access to dependable educational resources.

    The platform was founded by R. C. Fernando, an experienced English teacher and lecturer, a graduate in English of the University of Peradeniya with a Master of Arts in Linguistics from the University of Kelaniya and a TESOL qualification from the University of Peradeniya.
