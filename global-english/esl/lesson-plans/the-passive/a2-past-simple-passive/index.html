Past Simple Passive ESL Lesson Plan (A2, 60 Minutes)

A complete 60-minute A2 lesson on Past Simple Passive, built round the theme "Famous Places and Great Inventions".

  • 60 minutes

About this lesson

Who it is for. Elementary students who know the present simple passive and the past simple. They want to talk about history but say Sigiriya built in the 5th century or The telephone was invent by Bell.

Everything the lesson needs is on this page: the language analysis, the timed stages with the teacher's own words and instruction-checking questions, the differentiation, the printable handouts and the answer key.

At a glance

Level
CEFR A2 (Elementary)
Learners
Teenagers and adults
Length
60 minutes. For 45 minutes, run only "Information gap" of the two tasks in "Freer practice" and cut "Feedback and wrap-up" to board-based error correction.
Skills
Speaking
Language focus
Grammar: Past Simple Passive (was built, were invented)
Framework
PPP (presentation, practice, production)
Materials
Pictures of Sigiriya, a telephone, a light bulb and the Taj Mahal (or descriptions), Handout 1 (fact files), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (quiz cards A and B), Handout 4 (write your own quiz).
Preparation
About 10 minutes: print Handouts 1 to 4, one per learner, and read the language analysis. The answer key is for you, not the class.

Aims

Main aim. To present and practise the past simple passive (was / were + past participle), with and without by + agent, to talk about famous places and inventions.

Learning outcome. By the end of the lesson, students can write and ask quiz questions such as When was the telephone invented? Who was Sigiriya built by? and answer them in full sentences.

  • Subsidiary aim. To review irregular past participles.
  • Subsidiary aim. To read short fact files for specific information.
  • Subsidiary aim. To develop speaking through a history quiz.

Language analysis

What to know before the lesson: the meaning, form and pronunciation of the target language, and the problems learners usually have with it.

Form

SingularPlural
PositiveSigiriya was built in the 5th century.The first cars were made in Germany.
NegativeIt wasn't built by the British.Phones weren't invented in 1700.
QuestionWhen was the telephone invented?Where were the first cars made?

Form: notes

  • was / were + past participle.
  • by + agent when we want to say who did it: The telephone was invented by Alexander Graham Bell.
  • Question about the agent: Who was it invented by?

Meaning and use

  • We use the passive when the place or object is the topic, and the person is less important, unknown or obvious: The temple was built in 1592.
  • We add by + agent only when the person is important or interesting.

Pronunciation

  • Weak was /wəz/ and were /wə/: It was built /ɪt wəz bɪlt/.
  • -ed endings: invented /ɪd/, discovered /d/, painted /ɪd/.

Concept-checking questions

  • Sigiriya was built in the 5th century. Is this about the past or now? (The past.) · Did Sigiriya build itself? (No, people built it.) · What is the topic of the sentence: Sigiriya or the builders? (Sigiriya.)
  • The telephone was invented by Bell. Who invented it? (Bell.) · Why do we say by Bell? (Because the person is important.)

Anticipated problems and solutions

ProblemSolution
Missing was / were (Sigiriya built in ...).Board the two-part formula; drill.
Base verb or past simple instead of participle (was invent, was wrote).Participle list; quick-fire drill.
was with plurals.Singular / plural contrast in Exercise A.
Overusing by + agent (by people, by someone).Rule: only when the agent is important.

Board plan

Left: participlesCentre: grammarRight: pronunciation
build → built · invent → invented · discover → discovered · paint → painted · write → written · make → made · find → found · design → designed
Bell invented the telephone. (active) → The telephone was invented by Bell. (passive) · was / were + past participle · by + agent (only if important) · When was it built? · Who was it invented by?
was /wəz/ · were /wə/ · invented /ɪd/ · discovered /d/

CEFR A2 · 60 minutes · Teenagers and adults

Procedure: Past Simple Passive, step by step

Warm-up5 minutes · Pairs, then whole class

Aim: To engage students and activate knowledge.

Show the four pictures. Ask: "What do you know about these? How old are they?" Pairs discuss (2 minutes).

Take a few ideas. Don't correct yet.

Presentation20 minutes · Individual, pairs, whole class

Aim: To present the past simple passive in context and clarify meaning, form and pronunciation.

Part A: Reading (6 minutes)

Give out Handout 1. Say: "Read the fact files. Find one date and one name in each. Three minutes."

ICQs: "How many fact files?" (Four.)

Pairs compare. Students answer the four questions. Check as a class.

Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)

Say: "Underline was or were + another verb." Pairs compare. Elicit examples and board them.

Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)

Meaning. Board King Kashyapa built Sigiriya and Sigiriya was built in the 5th century. Ask the CCQs.

Form. Elicit was / were + past participle. Highlight were with plurals.

by + agent. Board The telephone was invented by Bell. Ask: "Why do we say by Bell here?" Contrast with The temple was built in 1592 (agent not needed).

Questions. Board When was the telephone invented? Who was it invented by?

Pronunciation. Drill weak forms: It was built in 1592. When was it invented? They were made in Germany.

Controlled practice10 minutes · Individual, then pairs, then whole class

Aim: To practise the form accurately.

Give out Handout 2. Say: "Exercise A: was or were + past participle. Exercise B: rewrite the sentences in the passive. Seven minutes."

Pairs check. Check as a class.

Freer practice18 minutes · Pairs, then teams

Aim: To use the past simple passive in a history quiz.

Information gap (8 minutes). Give Student A and Student B the quiz cards (Handout 3). Each card has answers the partner needs. Students ask and answer: "When was the first photograph taken?" "It was taken in 1826."

ICQs: "Can you show your card?" (No.) "Do you answer in a full sentence?" (Yes.)

Write a quiz (10 minutes). Give out Handout 4. Teams write five quiz questions about places and inventions in their own country or the world, then ask another team.

Monitor and note errors.

Feedback and wrap-up7 minutes · Whole class

Aim: To give feedback and consolidate.

Board four sentences heard in Stage 4 (two with errors). Students correct them.

Ask: "When do we use by?"

Set the homework.

Homework Write a fact file (80 words) about a famous place or invention. Use the past simple passive at least four times.

PPP (presentation, practice, production). Stage times add up to 60 minutes.

Differentiation

Support
Give weaker students the participle list during the quiz. Provide question frames: When was ___ built / invented? Who was ___ painted by?
Stretch
Stronger students add extra facts with the present simple passive: It is visited by thousands of tourists every year. They write negative sentences that correct false facts: The light bulb wasn't invented by Newton.

Adapting the lesson

ForWhat to change
A large class (40 or more)Keep the pairwork in "Freer practice" in fixed pairs rather than a mingle, and take feedback from four or five pairs instead of the whole room. Run "Information gap" once, with two pairs reporting, rather than hearing every pair. Monitor one half of the room during "Controlled practice" and the other half during "Freer practice".
A class with no photocopierPut the exercises from Handout 2: Practice on the board for learners to copy into their notebooks, and read any text aloud twice instead of printing it. Handout 3: Quiz Cards (Information Gap) works read out from the front: learners note what they need rather than holding a card. The board plan above is this lesson's minimum - with that on the board, it runs on no paper at all.
An online classSend Handouts 1 to 4 in the chat before the lesson and put the board plan on a shared slide. Use breakout rooms for "Information gap" and visit each room once. Have learners type the controlled answers in the chat, so you see every answer at once instead of nominating.
A different age groupWith young learners, cut the writing at the end and give that time to the speaking task; keep the drilling short, and let them draw or mime where the plan allows it. With adults, keep the writing and ask for a short spoken summary in place of anything drawn or mimed.

Checking learning

8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A. Students form past passive questions correctly in the quiz. Students use by + agent only when the agent is important.

Teacher's reflection

Did students use the past participle, not the past simple form? Did the quiz motivate accurate questions?

Handout 1: Fact Files

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

Sigiriya The rock fortress of Sigiriya was built in the 5th century by King Kashyapa. Beautiful paintings were made on the rock walls. Today, it is visited by thousands of tourists every year.

The Telephone The telephone was invented in 1876 by Alexander Graham Bell. The first words on the phone were spoken to his assistant: "Come here, I want to see you."

The Light Bulb The first electric lights were made in the early 1800s, but the first useful light bulb was designed by Thomas Edison in 1879.

The Taj Mahal The Taj Mahal was built in India between 1632 and 1653. It was made of white marble, and it was designed as a tomb for the emperor's wife.

Answer the questions.

  • Who was Sigiriya built by? ______________________________
  • When was the telephone invented? ______________________________
  • Who was the first useful light bulb designed by? ______________________________
  • What was the Taj Mahal made of? ______________________________

Handout 2: Practice

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

A. Complete with was or were and the past participle.

B. Rewrite the sentences in the passive. Use by only if the person is important.

  • The Temple of the Tooth ______ ______________ (build) in the 16th century.
  • The first cars ______ ______________ (make) in Germany.
  • The book Harry Potter ______ ______________ (write) by J. K. Rowling.
  • Penicillin ______ ______________ (discover) in 1928.
  • These photos ______ ______________ (take) in 1950.
  • Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa. → ______________________________
  • Someone stole my bike yesterday. → ______________________________
  • The Wright brothers built the first plane. → ______________________________
