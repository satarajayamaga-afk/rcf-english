Getting started

How to Use This Site

RCF English holds a great deal of material. This page shows you how it is arranged and how to find what you need quickly.

  1. Home
  2. How to Use This Site

How the site is arranged

There are four learning sections, one resource centre, one section for teachers, and one for classes.

The four learning sections

O/L English, O/L Literature, General English and A/L Literature. Each one has a landing page that explains the subject, and a menu of lessons underneath it.

Past Papers

The Past Papers centre holds past papers, model papers, marking schemes, revision papers and question banks, with filters for subject, year, paper and type.

Teacher Resources

Teacher Resources holds lesson plans, worksheets, marking guides and support for mixed-ability and remedial teaching.

RCF Classes

RCF Classes describes the online classes conducted by R. C. Fernando, and how to ask about them on WhatsApp.

Three ways to find something

  1. Use the menu

    Every main section opens a menu of its pages. On a telephone, tap Menu, then tap the arrow beside a section to open it.

  2. Use search

    Search covers everything on the site. Every result tells you whether it is a lesson, a past paper, an activity, a class or a policy.

  3. Follow the trail

    Every page shows a trail at the top telling you where you are, and a link back to the section you came from.

If you are a student

Start with the syllabus page

Know the shape of the paper before you revise. It tells you what to spend your time on.

O/L English syllabus

Learn writing step by step

Never begin a writing task by writing. Understand it, study a model, find the structure, then write.

Guided writing

Practise, then check

Do an activity, read every explanation, and repeat it a few days later.

Practical questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Nothing on RCF English requires an account, a login or a password. The interactive activities run inside your own browser.

Does the site work on a telephone?

Yes. Every page is designed for small screens first, including the timetable, which appears as cards rather than a wide table on a telephone.

Will it work on a slow connection?

It should. The pages are plain files with no heavy libraries, no video and no tracking scripts, so they load quickly even on a weak signal.

Can I print a lesson?

Yes. Use your browser's print command. The menus, buttons and footer are removed automatically so that only the lesson is printed.

Is everything free?

Everything on RCF English is free to read and use. Books and study guides are sold separately through RCF Publications, which is a different website.
