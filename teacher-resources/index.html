Material you can take into a classroom without rewriting it first, written by a teacher who has had to teach the same lessons to thirty-eight students at four different levels.

What this section is for

Most teaching material assumes conditions that do not exist: a small class, a single level, a photocopier, and time to prepare. This section assumes the opposite, because that is what most English classrooms in Sri Lanka actually look like.

The emphasis throughout is on things that work with large mixed-ability classes, few materials and limited preparation time.
