Using these resources

  • Material created by RCF English may be printed, copied and used freely for teaching, with acknowledgement.
  • It may not be republished as your own work, sold, or included in anything sold, without written permission.

Guidance available now as pages

Every page below is complete and can be used or printed today, whether or not a downloadable file exists yet.

  • Lesson Plans — a lesson structure and four worked plans
  • Worksheets — how to design worksheets that teach
  • Teaching Guides — how to teach the difficult topics
