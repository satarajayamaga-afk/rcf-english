Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English) PPP Lesson Plans

20 ready-to-teach sample plans that follow the Advanced Level General English textbook (one book for Grades 12 and 13).

Word document (.docx) · PDF

The Word file can be edited in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, WPS Office or LibreOffice, so you can add your school's name and change it for your class. The PDF is ready to print.

About these plans

Grades 12 and 13 share one General English textbook with eight units, so there is no separate Grade 12 or Grade 13 book. These twenty plans (ten for each year) follow the eight units in order. Your school's scheme decides which units fall in Grade 12 and which in Grade 13. Stage times are for a 40-minute period.

Every plan on this page was written by RCF English. The unit and activity names refer to the government Pupil's Book so that you can find the right page; no textbook text is reproduced here. Download it as an editable Word document or a print-ready PDF.

The PPP stages in one line each

Presentation: the teacher shows the new language in a clear context. Practice: students use it in controlled tasks, usually from the Pupil's Book. Production: students use it more freely for their own purpose. Check and close: a quick check of learning, then homework.

Plan 1: Unit 1, Sri Lankan Achievers

Book activity: Reading 1 (an everyday hero). Focus: Reading for gist and detail.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to identify the main idea of a story and explain why a person is admired.

Materials: The Reading 1 text.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 1: Reading for gist and detail. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minPairwork from the book: think of someone you admire and give two reasons. Pre-teach key words from the story.
Practice12 minRead for gist, then answer the detail questions.
Production13 minGroups write a short profile of an everyday hero from their own village.
Check and close5 minShare one profile. Homework: Write a 150-word paragraph about someone you admire.

Plan 2: Unit 1, Sri Lankan Achievers

Book activity: Listening 1; Writing. Focus: Listening for specific information.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to note key facts from a talk and write a short profile of an achiever.

Materials: The listening text (download from edupub.gov.lk), the achievers table.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 1: Listening for specific information. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minDiscuss the famous Sri Lankans in Before Listening. Show how to take notes in a table.
Practice12 minListen and complete the notes.
Production13 minComplete the achievements table (Writing) and write a short profile of one achiever.
Check and close5 minPeer-check facts. Homework: Research another Sri Lankan achiever.

Plan 3: Unit 2, Living in Harmony

Book activity: Speaking and Writing. Focus: Describing a picture and people.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to describe a picture and a friend using appropriate adjectives.

Materials: The Unit 2 picture.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 2: Describing a picture and people. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minDescribe the picture with the class: what are the children feeling? Show useful phrases: S/he likes ... S/he thinks ...
Practice12 minPairs describe the picture.
Production13 minWrite a short description of a friend using the sentence guide.
Check and close5 minRead two descriptions. Homework: Write about harmony in your school.

Plan 4: Unit 2, Living in Harmony

Book activity: Grammar: conditionals and modal verbs. Focus: Conditionals and modals.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to use conditional sentences and modal verbs to talk about coexistence.

Materials: The harmony quotation.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 2: Conditionals and modals. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minRead the quotation: If there is righteousness in the heart ... Show the first and second conditional forms.
Practice12 minComplete the grammar activities.
Production13 minGroups write five rules for living in harmony using modals.
Check and close5 minShare the rules. Homework: Write a paragraph using three conditionals.

Plan 5: Unit 3, Relationships

Book activity: Speaking: quotations. Focus: Discussing meaning.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to discuss the meaning of quotations and give reasons.

Materials: The quotations.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 3: Discussing meaning. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minRead the quotations. Show discussion phrases: I think it means ...
Practice12 minGroups discuss the meanings.
Production13 minEach group presents one interpretation.
Check and close5 minClass votes on the best interpretation. Homework: Write a quotation of your own and explain it.

Plan 6: Unit 3, Relationships

Book activity: Writing: a story. Focus: Process writing.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to write a story through prewriting, drafting, feedback and revising.

Materials: The process-writing steps.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 3: Process writing. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minExplain the steps in the book. Model prewriting with a mind map.
Practice12 minPupils prewrite and draft.
Production13 minPeer feedback and revision.
Check and close5 minShare one story. Homework: Finish the final draft.

Plan 7: Unit 3, Relationships

Book activity: Speaking: mini debates; Writing: essay. Focus: Debating and a discursive essay.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to take part in a mini debate and write a 250-word essay discussing both sides.

Materials: The debate topics.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 3: Debating and a discursive essay. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minExplain proposition and opposition. Show essay structure: introduction, both sides, conclusion.
Practice12 minMini debates in groups.
Production13 minWrite a 250-word essay on one topic.
Check and close5 minPeer-check structure. Homework: Finish the essay.

Plan 8: Unit 4, English as a Local and Global Language

Book activity: Speaking and Writing 1. Focus: Discussing advantages and difficulties.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to list and explain the advantages and difficulties of learning English.

Materials: The discussion questions.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 4: Discussing advantages and difficulties. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minDiscuss the two questions. Show how to support a point with an example.
Practice12 minGroups make a common list and select the six best points.
Production13 minWrite a paragraph with examples.
Check and close5 minShare one paragraph. Homework: Write about your own English learning.

Plan 9: Unit 4, English as a Local and Global Language

Book activity: Speaking 3; Language focus. Focus: Describing feelings and hopes.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to describe a picture and express feelings and hopes.

Materials: The rural English class picture.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 4: Describing feelings and hopes. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minDescribe the picture. Show phrases: I hope ... I wish ...
Practice12 minPairwork: imagine being a student in the picture.
Production13 minWrite a short paragraph about hopes for learning English.
Check and close5 minRead one paragraph. Homework: Write about your ideal English class.

Plan 10: Unit 4, English as a Local and Global Language

Book activity: Grammar: future tenses. Focus: Future forms.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to use will, going to and the present continuous for the future.

Materials: Future forms table.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 4: Future forms. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minShow the three future forms. Contrast plans and predictions.
Practice12 minComplete the grammar activity.
Production13 minPairs talk about their plans after A/L.
Check and close5 minShare one plan. Homework: Write about your plans.

Plan 11: Unit 5, Crime and Social Responsibility

Book activity: Speaking 1; Reading 1. Focus: Vocabulary of crime.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to understand crime vocabulary and summarise news items.

Materials: The dictionary definitions, the news items.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 5: Vocabulary of crime. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minDiscuss the definitions of crime. Pre-teach vocabulary.
Practice12 minRead the six news items and answer the questions.
Production13 minSummarise one news item for the class.
Check and close5 minShare summaries. Homework: Find a news item and summarise it.

Plan 12: Unit 5, Crime and Social Responsibility

Book activity: Reading 2. Focus: Guessing meaning from context.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to guess the meaning of words from context.

Materials: Laws in different countries.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 5: Guessing meaning from context. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minShow strategies for guessing meaning. Read the first description together.
Practice12 minRead and guess the bold words.
Production13 minDiscuss which law is most surprising and why.
Check and close5 minShare views. Homework: Write about a law you would change.

Plan 13: Unit 5, Crime and Social Responsibility

Book activity: Grammar: direct and reported speech. Focus: Reported speech.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to change direct speech into reported speech.

Materials: The monastery passage.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 5: Reported speech. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minRead the passage. Show the tense and pronoun changes.
Practice12 minComplete the grammar activity.
Production13 minReport a conversation from the passage.
Check and close5 minCheck tense changes. Homework: Report a conversation from home.

Plan 14: Unit 6, The Cyber World

Book activity: Reading 1; Writing emails. Focus: Formal emails.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, students will be able to write a formal email with the correct structure and tone.

Grades 12 and 13 (A/L General English), Unit 6: Formal emails. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
