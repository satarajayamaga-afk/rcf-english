Grade 7 PPP Lesson Plans

10 ready-to-teach sample plans that follow the Grade 7 English Pupil's Book.

  2. English Teachers Resources
  3. PPP Lesson Plans
  4. Grade 7 PPP Lesson Plans

Download this page

Word document (.docx) · PDF

The Word file can be edited in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, WPS Office or LibreOffice, so you can add your school's name and change it for your class. The PDF is ready to print.

About these plans

One plan for each of the ten units. Stage times are for a 40-minute period.

Every plan on this page was written by RCF English. The unit and activity names refer to the government Pupil's Book so that you can find the right page; no textbook text is reproduced here. Download it as an editable Word document or a print-ready PDF.

The PPP stages in one line each

Presentation: the teacher shows the new language in a clear context. Practice: pupils use it in controlled tasks, usually from the Pupil's Book. Production: pupils use it more freely for their own purpose. Check and close: a quick check of learning, then homework.

Plan 1: Unit 1, What You See

Book activity: Activities 3, 4 and 5. Focus: Opposites and synonyms.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to give the opposite of common adjectives and pick out words with similar meanings.

Materials: Picture pairs (big / small, hot / cold), word cards.

Grade 7, Unit 1: Opposites and synonyms. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minShow picture pairs and elicit the opposites. Explain synonyms with warm – hot and happy – glad.
Practice12 minWrite opposites under the pictures (Activity 3) and match opposites (Activity 4). Circle the two synonyms in each set (Activity 5).
Production13 minPairs describe a classmate's bag with three adjectives; the partner replies with the opposites or a synonym.
Check and close5 minOpposites race: teams of two, teacher calls a word. Homework: Write ten opposite pairs of your own.

Plan 2: Unit 2, Friends Indeed

Book activity: Activities 1, 2 and 3. Focus: Reading a story and writing instructions.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to answer questions on Fifi's story and write clear instructions in order.

Materials: Fifi's story, instruction strips cut up for ordering.

Grade 7, Unit 2: Reading a story and writing instructions. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minPre-teach glass, spacious and freely, then read Fifi's story aloud. Show how instructions begin with a verb and use then, next.
Practice12 minAnswer the Activity 1 questions in pairs. Put the cut-up instructions in order (Activity 2).
Production13 minGroups design a simple anti-dengue poster and write four instructions to go with it (Activity 3).
Check and close5 minGroups show their posters. Homework: Write five instructions for looking after a pet.

Plan 3: Unit 3, Pleasure

Book activity: Activities 1, 5 and 7. Focus: Was, were and will be.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to use was / were for the past and will be for the future when telling a story.

Materials: The Perahera dialogue, the Merryland story.

Grade 7, Unit 3: Was, were and will be. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minAct out the Perahera dialogue with two pupils. Draw a timeline: yesterday – was / were, tomorrow – will be.
Practice12 minFill in the Merryland story with was, were or will be (Activity 5). Listen and match letters to the playground passage (Activity 7).
Production13 minPairs tell each other about a festival they saw and one they will see.
Check and close5 minThree pupils report their partner's answers. Homework: Write six sentences about a festival, past and future.

Plan 4: Unit 4, A Busy Day

Book activity: Activities 1, 3 and 9. Focus: Reading a notice and possessive pronouns.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to find information in a notice and use mine, yours, his, hers, ours and theirs.

Materials: The scout notice, pupils' own pens and books.

Grade 7, Unit 4: Reading a notice and possessive pronouns. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minAct out Lahiru's busy day. Pick up a pupil's pen: Is this yours? Yes, it's mine. Build the list.
Practice12 minComplete the table from the notice (Activity 3). Fill in the possessive pronouns (Activity 9).
Production13 minLost-property game: the teacher collects objects and pupils claim them using the pronouns.
Check and close5 minCorrect: This book is her's → hers. Homework: Write a notice for a class event.

Plan 5: Unit 5, Once Upon a Time

Book activity: Activities 3, 4 and 8. Focus: Joining sentences with and, but, or and when.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to combine short sentences using and, but, or and when.

Materials: Sentence strips about early people.

Grade 7, Unit 5: Joining sentences with and, but, or and when. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minShow two strips: Early people lived in caves. They hunted animals. Join them with and. Contrast and, but, or and when with examples.
Practice12 minCombine the sentences (Activity 3). Join sentences with when (Activity 8).
Production13 minGroups speak about the journey of human civilisation, each pupil giving one joined sentence (Activity 4).
Check and close5 minNote the best joined sentences on the board. Homework: Write a short paragraph about a museum visit using when twice.

Plan 6: Unit 6, Better Safe Than Sorry

Book activity: Activities 1, 3 and 4. Focus: Must, should, would and can.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to give advice and rules with must and should and write a short note.

Materials: The Suganya dialogue, a get-well card.

Grade 7, Unit 6: Must, should, would and can. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minAct out Suganya is Absent Today. Write rules and advice: You must wash your hands. You should rest.
Practice12 minComplete the sentences with must, should, would or can (Activity 3).
Production13 minPupils write Suganya's thank-you note to her friends (Activity 4).
Check and close5 minRead two notes aloud. Homework: Make a get-well card for someone you know.

Plan 7: Unit 7, Around the Country

Book activity: Activities 1, 6 and 8. Focus: Adverbs and speaking about places.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to use adverbs to describe how things happen and speak for one minute about a place.

Materials: Ruvini's speech, A Trip to Jaffna, adverb cards.

Grade 7, Unit 7: Adverbs and speaking about places. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minRead Ruvini's speech. Pupils answer the Activity 1 questions. Show adverbs: The train went slowly. They arrived safely.
Practice12 minFill in the adverbs (Activity 6).
Production13 minPupils choose a place and speak about it for a minute (Activity 8).
Check and close5 minClass gives one compliment to each speaker. Homework: Write a description of a place you visited.

Plan 8: Unit 8, Wonders Around Us

Book activity: Activities 2, 3 and 6. Focus: Reading for information and daily schedules.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to take notes in a table from descriptions and talk about their daily schedule.

Materials: Pictures of famous monuments, a blank table.

Grade 7, Unit 8: Reading for information and daily schedules. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minShow the monuments and ask what pupils know. Model a schedule: 6.30 a.m. – I have breakfast.
Practice12 minMatch descriptions and pictures (Activity 2) and complete the table (Activity 3).
Production13 minPupils write their own schedule and compare it with a partner (Activity 6).
Check and close5 minPairs report one difference. Homework: Find one fact about a Sri Lankan monument.

Plan 9: Unit 9, Our Beautiful World

Book activity: Activities 4, 5 and 6. Focus: Prepositions of movement.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to use up, down, over, into, below and next to to describe a picture.

Materials: The Unit 9 picture, a toy car or ball.

Grade 7, Unit 9: Prepositions of movement. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minMove a toy: It goes up the hill. It goes over the bridge. Write the prepositions with arrows.
Practice12 minComplete the sentences about the pictures (Activity 4).
Production13 minDescribe the picture with the given words (Activity 5), then do the describe-and-draw task (Activity 6).
Check and close5 minCompare drawings with the originals. Homework: Write five sentences about your way to school.

Plan 10: Unit 10, Future

Book activity: Activities 2, 3 and 7. Focus: Predictions with will and won't.

Learning outcome: by the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to make and discuss predictions about life in the future.

Materials: The BBC predictions text.

Grade 7, Unit 10: Predictions with will and won't. A 40-minute PPP lesson.
StageTimeWhat happens
Presentation10 minRead two predictions and ask Will this come true? Show will / won't + verb.
Practice12 minMake sentences with will / won't (Activity 7). Discuss the predictions (Activity 2).
Production13 minGroups write and present a prediction for one category (Activity 3).
Check and close5 minClass votes on the most likely prediction. Homework: Write five predictions about your village in 2050.
