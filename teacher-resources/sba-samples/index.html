Sample School Based Assessment (SBA) Tools for English and Literature

Ready-to-use assessment tools in the familiar school format: details, instructions, marking criteria and signatures.

  1. Home
  2. English Teachers Resources
  3. Sample School Based Assessment (SBA) Tools for English and Literature

Download this page

Word document (.docx) · PDF

The Word file can be edited in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, WPS Office or LibreOffice, so you can add your school's name and change it for your class. The PDF is ready to print.

These are samples, not official instructions

Each tool is set out in the usual School Based Assessment tool format. Fill in your school's name and the date. For Grades 6 to 11 English, the competency and competency level numbers are taken from the NIE English Language Teachers' Guides for those grades (see Competency Levels by Grade). The Grade 6 guide used is the edition implemented from 2015, so schools on the new 2026 Grade 6 syllabus should use the numbers in the new guide. For primary grades, A/L General English and Literature we do not yet have the Teachers' Guides, so the competency wording there is a sample: copy the official competency and level from your guide. Follow the SBA plan and circulars for your grade and year for the number of tools, their timing and how marks are recorded. The terms shown in the Literature tools are suggestions; change them to suit your school's plan.

English: Primary (Grades 2 to 5)

SBA plan 1: Show and tell

Grade2 / 3
SubjectEnglish
Date______________________
Term1st / 2nd / 3rd (as set in your school's SBA plan)
Selected evaluationOral presentation
Time duration1 to 2 minutes per pupil (over two periods)
CompetencyUses simple spoken English to talk about familiar things.
Competency levelNames and describes a familiar object in simple sentences. (Sample wording: use the competency level in your Teacher's Guide.)
Subject contentThings at home and in school; colours and sizes
Nature of the toolIndividual oral presentation
Learning outcomePupils will be able to say three simple sentences about an object they bring from home.
Teacher's activityModels a short show-and-tell with an object, gives the sentence frames (This is my ... It is ... I like it because ...), and calls pupils one by one.
Students' activityBring an object from home and say at least three sentences about it to the class.
