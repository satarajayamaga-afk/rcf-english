# Resource publishing policy

**Read this before adding any past paper, marking scheme, worksheet or literary text
to RCF English.**

This is the working rule for the site. A shortened version for visitors is published
at `/copyright-policy/`; this document is the one for whoever maintains the site.

---

## The rule

A resource may be published on RCF English only if **at least one** of these is true:

1. **RCF English created it.**
2. **The copyright holder has given permission** for it to be distributed.
3. **R. C. Fernando has personally approved** its publication.
4. **It can lawfully be linked — not copied —** from the official website that
   publishes it.

If none of the four applies, it is not published. Not "probably fine", not "everyone
else has it", not "it was on a WhatsApp group". Not published.

---

## The point that matters most

> **A file existing in Google Drive is not permission to publish it.**

Neither is any of the following:

- It was forwarded to you by another teacher.
- It is already on three other websites.
- It has no copyright notice printed on it.
- It is old.
- It is being given away free.
- A search engine already indexes it.

Copyright exists whether or not it is claimed. The absence of a notice is not
permission, and other people's infringement is not a defence.

---

## What we publish

- Lessons, explanations and guidance written by RCF English
- Model questions and model answers written by RCF English
- Worksheets, revision sheets and lesson plans created by RCF English
- Papers whose copyright holder has given permission
- **Links** to past papers on the official websites that publish them
- Short quotations from literary works, for study and criticism, with the author and
  the work named

## What we do not publish

- Copyrighted past papers copied without permission
- Complete copyrighted poems, stories, plays or novels
- Complete paid eBooks, or any book belonging to a publisher
- Marking schemes we are not entitled to distribute
- Anyone's private files, correspondence or personal data
- Payment details, bank details or identity documents
- Unfinished manuscripts or drafts
- Duplicate copies of something already on the site
- Anything whose origin is uncertain

## The Google Drive procedure

Approved resources are kept in a Google Drive folder named:

**`RCF Website – Approved Resources`**

Suggested sub-folders:

```
RCF Website – Approved Resources/
├─ O-L-English/
├─ O-L-Literature/
├─ General-English/
├─ A-L-Literature/
├─ Past-Papers/
├─ Model-Papers/
├─ Marking-Schemes/
├─ Teacher-Resources/
└─ Free-Samples/
```

### Rules for that folder

1. **Only that one folder is ever used.** The rest of the Drive is never searched,
   scanned or published from — not by a person, not by a script, not by an assistant.
2. **Putting a file in that folder is a decision.** The folder means "approved for
   publication", not "might be useful one day". Keep unsorted material elsewhere.
3. **Nothing is published without a review first** (below).

### The review, before anything is published

For every file, write down these seven things and look at them together:

| | |
|---|---|
| **1. Filename** | `OL-Eng-2019-P1.pdf` |
| **2. Proposed website title** | O/L English 2019, Paper I |
| **3. Category** | Past Papers |
| **4. Resource type** | `past-paper` |
| **5. Proposed location** | `past-papers/ol-english/` |
| **6. Copyright status** | Created by RCF English / Published with permission / Linked from the official source |
| **7. Copied or linked?** | Copied into `downloads/` — or linked to the official address |

Then decide. If line 6 cannot be completed honestly, the answer is no.

**Nothing is published until this review has been done and approved.** If you are
working with an assistant, the assistant presents this list and waits. It does not
publish first and ask afterwards.

### Never upload to the website

- Complete paid eBooks
- Personal files
- Private correspondence
- Payment or bank details
- Unfinished manuscripts
- Duplicate files
- Copyrighted material without permission

## Literary texts

Literature teaching needs the text; copyright law protects it. The line RCF English
works to:

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Published freely** | Summaries, plot outlines, character notes, themes, analysis, context questions, essay questions, model answers, revision exercises — all written by RCF English |
| ✅ **Published with care** | Short quotations, used for study and criticism, with the author and work named |
| ❌ **Never published** | Complete copyrighted poems, stories, plays or novels, or large parts of them |

Where a text is **out of copyright**, longer extracts may be given. Where it is still
in copyright, the study material stands on its own and the student is expected to
have the prescribed book. Every text page says so.

### Accuracy is part of this

- **Never invent a quotation.** If you cannot check it against the book, refer to the
  moment instead: *"when she refuses the money at the station"*.
- **Never invent a fact about an author** — a date, a place, a biographical detail.
- **Never invent a critic or a critical opinion.** *"It has often been argued that…"*
  is honest and sufficient.
- If you are not certain, leave it out. An empty section is better than a wrong one.

## Marking schemes

An official marking scheme belongs to the body that produced it.

- Publish one **only** where the copyright holder has permitted it.
- Otherwise, **link** to the official source where the official source publishes it.
- Marking **guidance written by RCF English** is original work and may be published
  freely — but it must be clearly distinguished from any official scheme, and never
  presented as one.

## School and provincial papers

A term-test paper belongs to the body that set it — a provincial department, a
zonal or divisional office, or a school.

### The decision that governs the Past Papers section

Sri Lankan provincial and zonal papers almost always carry **"All Rights
Reserved"** printed on them, in Sinhala, Tamil and English. They also circulate
openly on educational websites across the country.

R. C. Fernando has decided that this site may list papers set by a **provincial
department, a zonal or divisional office, or a school**, on these conditions:

- The site **links** to the file in Google Drive. It never hosts a copy, and no
  PDF is committed to this repository.
- The body printed on the paper is **recorded on the paper's card**, exactly as
  printed.
- The site **never** describes a provincial, zonal or school paper as something
  RCF English created.

This is a considered decision about a real and low risk, not a claim that
permission has been granted. Linking to a file is a lighter act than
republishing one. If a department ever objects, remove the paper at once and
follow *If a complaint arrives* below.

### Private institutes are excluded

Papers produced by **private institutes, academies, tuition classes and
publishers must not be listed at all.** They are commercial products and the
argument above does not apply to them.

**The publisher is rarely in the filename.** Files named "Thondamannaru
Division" turned out to be *Thondamanaru Thanu Publications*, a commercial
publisher whose name appears only on the page itself — and which reprints
provincial papers in paid compilations. **Every paper must be opened and read
before it is added.** A filename search is not enough.

Excluded on this ground so far: Thondamanaru Thanu Publications, Oxford
Publications, Oxford International, Panacea English Class, Sumex mock papers,
CEETS Center and Colombo Resource Center.

### When a teacher offers a school's papers

- Ask them to confirm they are entitled to give that permission. Most are; some
  are not, and it is a fair question that protects them as well as the site.
- Record who gave permission and when.

## Material created by RCF English

Lessons, guidance, model answers, worksheets and activities on the site are RCF
English's own work unless a different author is named.

**Others may:** use and print it for teaching, learning and revision, with
acknowledgement.

**Others may not:** republish it as their own, sell it, or include it in a commercial
product without written permission.

Books published by RCF Publications are sold separately and are **not** covered by
this permission.

## If a complaint arrives

1. **Take it seriously and act quickly.** A genuine complaint is a chance to put
   something right, not an argument to win.
2. **Take the material down while the position is checked.** Do not leave it up and
   debate. Set `"published": false` in the data file, run `build.cmd`, commit and
   push — that removes it from the site in about two minutes.
3. **Ask for the details** listed on the `/corrections/` page: the exact address, a
   description of the material, the work and the owner, and how they would like it
   resolved.
4. **Decide honestly.** If it should not have been published, apologise and leave it
   down. If a link and an acknowledgement will satisfy the owner, offer that.
5. **Record what happened**, so the same mistake is not repeated.

---

## What the site must never claim

- **No government endorsement.** RCF English is independent and is not endorsed by,
  approved by or affiliated with any government department, examination authority or
  public institution.
- **No official status for model answers.** They are teaching material written by RCF
  English. They are not official answers and are not the only acceptable ones.
- **No guaranteed results.** No examination outcome is promised, implied or hinted at.
- **No invented qualifications, awards, statistics, student numbers or testimonials.**
  Only what is true and can be supported.

---

## A short test before publishing anything

Ask yourself these four questions. If any answer is uncomfortable, do not publish.

1. **Who owns this, and how do I know I may publish it?**
2. **If the owner saw it here tomorrow, would they be content?**
3. **Is everything on this page actually true, and could I show where it came from?**
4. **Would I be willing to explain this decision in public?**
