# Reported Questions, Requests and Commands (asked if, told me to)

*Lesson theme: The Job Interview*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Reported Speech |
| Grammar point | Reported Questions, Requests and Commands (asked if, told me to) |
| Level | B1 (Intermediate) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Intermediate students who report statements with tense changes. When they report questions they keep question word order and auxiliaries (She asked me where did I live), and they report commands with said (He said me to sit down). |
| Main aim | To help students work out and use reported wh- questions, yes / no questions (if / whether) and reported requests and commands (asked / told + object + (not) to + infinitive). |
| Subsidiary aims | To develop fluency in a job interview role-play; to practise intonation in direct questions. |

## Language analysis

### Form

| **Type** | **Direct speech** | **Reported speech** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Wh- question | "Where do you live?" | She asked me where I lived. |
| Wh- question | "Why do you want this job?" | She asked (me) why I wanted the job. |
| Yes / no question | "Can you work at weekends?" | She asked if / whether I could work at weekends. |
| Request | "Could you send your references, please?" | She asked me to send my references. |
| Command | "Sit down." / "Don't be late." | She told me to sit down. / She told me not to be late. |

- Reported questions use **statement word order** (subject + verb) and **no do / does / did**. No question mark.
- *Yes / no* questions use **if** or **whether**.
- Requests and commands: **ask / tell + object + (not) to + infinitive**.
- Tenses move back as with statements.

### Meaning and use

- *ask* is used for questions and polite requests; *tell* is used for orders and instructions.

### Pronunciation

- Direct *wh-* questions fall at the end: *Where do you live? ↘*. *Yes / no* questions rise: *Can you work at weekends? ↗*. Reported questions are statements, so they fall: *She asked if I could work at weekends ↘.*

### Concept checking questions

*She asked me where I lived.*

1. Is this a question now? (No, it's a statement about a question.)
2. Is there a question mark? (No.) Is there *do / did*? (No.)

*She told me not to be late.*

1. Is this a question or an order? (An order.)
2. What did she actually say? (Don't be late.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Question word order (She asked where did I live). | Board: asked + where + SUBJECT + VERB; cross out did. |
| Missing if (She asked could I work). | Rule: no question word → if / whether. |
| said for commands (He said me to sit down). | Board: tell / ask + person + to + infinitive. |
| not in the wrong place (She told me don't be late). | Board: told me not to be late. |

## Board plan

| **Left: reporting verbs** | **Centre: patterns** | **Right: pronunciation** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ask · tell · want to know · wonder · order · remind | "Where do you live?" → She asked me where I lived. · asked + wh- + S + V (no do, no ?) · "Can you ...?" → She asked if / whether I could ... · "Please send ..." → She asked me to send ... · "Don't be late." → She told me not to be late. · ask / tell + person + (not) to + infinitive | Where do you live? ↘ · Can you work? ↗ · She asked if I could work ↘ |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To introduce the topic and personalise it. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Ask: *"Have you ever had an interview: for a job, a school or a club? What did they ask you?"*
2. Pairs list four typical interview questions (2 minutes). Board a few.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present reported questions, requests and commands in context and help students work out the rules. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the job interview. Then read Ishan's message to his friend. Did he get the job?"* (3 minutes.)
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the three questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Finding the rules (7 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Compare the interview and Ishan's message. Answer the questions with your partner."*
   - ICQs: *"Alone or with a partner?"* (With a partner.) *"Which two texts do you compare?"* (The interview and the message.)
2. Monitor. Ask stuck pairs: *"Where is the verb in the reported question? Where is did?"*

**Part C: Clarification (7 minutes)**

1. Elicit the answers and build the patterns in the centre of the board.
2. Ask the CCQs from the Language analysis.
3. Highlight: statement word order, no *do / did*, no question mark; *if / whether* for *yes / no* questions; *ask / tell + person + (not) to*.
4. **Pronunciation.** Contrast intonation: *Can you work at weekends? ↗* vs *She asked if I could work at weekends ↘.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the forms accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 3. Say: *"Exercise A: report the questions. Exercise B: report the requests and commands. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use reported questions and commands fluently after a role-play. **Interaction:** Pairs, then new pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 4. Student A is an interviewer for a summer job; Student B is the candidate. A asks at least six questions and gives two instructions at the end (*Bring your ID card on Monday. Don't forget ...*). Swap roles with a different job (10 minutes).
   - ICQs: *"How many questions?"* (At least six.) *"What do you give at the end?"* (Two instructions.)
2. **Report (6 minutes).** Students change partners and report their interview: *"She asked me why I wanted the job. She asked if I had any experience. She told me to bring my ID card."*
3. The class decides which candidates got the jobs (2 minutes).
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board sentences from Stage 4 (correct and incorrect). Students correct the errors.
2. Ask: *"What three things change when we report a question?"* (Word order, no *do / did*, no question mark.)
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write a message (120 words) to a friend about an interview, a visit to the doctor or a meeting with a teacher. Report at least four questions and two instructions.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the patterns on a card.
- In the role-play, provide the interviewer with a list of questions.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *wanted to know, wondered* and *reminded me to*.
- They report questions politely in the present: *Could you tell me where the station is?* (indirect questions preview).

## Assessment and success criteria

- In the report, students use statement word order in reported questions.
- Students use *if / whether* for *yes / no* questions.
- 9 out of 11 or better on Handout 3.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students stop using *did* in reported questions?
- Was the role-play realistic enough to produce instructions?

# Handout 1: The Job Interview

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**The interview at Ocean View Hotel, Bentota**

**Mrs Jayawardena:** Please sit down. Where do you live, Ishan? **Ishan:** In Kalutara. **Mrs Jayawardena:** Why do you want this job? **Ishan:** I love meeting people, and I want to improve my English. **Mrs Jayawardena:** Have you worked in a hotel before? **Ishan:** No, but I worked in my uncle's shop last summer. **Mrs Jayawardena:** Can you work at weekends? **Ishan:** Yes, I can. **Mrs Jayawardena:** Good. Could you send me two references, please? And don't be late on your first day!

**Ishan's message to his friend**

Hi Dilan! I had my interview today. First, the manager told me to sit down. She asked me where I lived and why I wanted the job. Then she asked if I had worked in a hotel before. I told her about Uncle's shop! She also asked whether I could work at weekends. At the end, she asked me to send two references and told me not to be late on my first day. So I think I've got the job!
