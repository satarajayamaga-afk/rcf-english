GRAMMAR TOPIC: QUESTION FORMS · A1 (Beginner) · 60 minutes

# Yes / No and Wh- Questions with Be and Do (Present Simple)

*Lesson theme: Getting to Know You*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Question Forms |
| Grammar point | Yes / No and Wh- Questions with Be and Do (Present Simple) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Beginners who use be and the present simple in statements. In questions they use statement word order with rising intonation (You are from Kandy?), forget do / does (Where you live?) or mix be and do (Do you are a student?). |
| Main aim | To present and practise yes / no and wh- questions with be and with do / does in the present simple, with short answers. |
| Subsidiary aims | To review question words (what, where, when, who, how old, how many); to practise question intonation; to develop speaking through a "find a friend" interview. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can ask and answer 8 personal questions, e.g. Where are you from? Do you have a pet? What time do you get up? |
| Materials | Handout 1 (a student's profile and interview), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (interview card), question-word cards. |

## Language analysis

### Form

| **Verb** | **Yes / no question** | **Short answers** | **Wh- question** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| be | Are you a student? Is she from Galle? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. · Yes, she is. / No, she isn't. | Where are you from? How old is he? |
| other verbs (I / you / we / they) | Do you like cricket? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. | What do you do at weekends? |
| other verbs (he / she / it) | Does she play the guitar? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. | Where does he live? |

- Word order: **(question word) + be + subject ...?** / **(question word) + do / does + subject + base verb ...?**
- After *does*, the main verb has **no -s**: *Does she* ***play?*** (not *Does she plays?*)
- Don't use *do* with *be*: *Are you ...?* (not *Do you are ...?*)

### Question words

*what* (thing), *where* (place), *when / what time* (time), *who* (person), *how old* (age), *how many* (number), *why* (reason)

### Pronunciation

- Yes / no questions usually **rise** at the end: *Do you like cricket? ↗*
- Wh- questions usually **fall**: *Where do you live? ↘*
- Weak *do you* /djə/: *Where do you live?* /weə djə lɪv/.

### Concept checking questions

*Does she play the guitar?*

1. Is this a question about one person or many? (One person, she.)
2. Is there *-s* on *play*? (No, because *does* has the *-s*.)

*Where are you from?*

1. Is the answer yes / no or a place? (A place.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Statement word order (You are from Kandy?). | Board arrows showing be / do moving to the front. |
| Missing do / does (Where you live?). | Colour-code the auxiliary; drill. |
| do with be (Do you are ...?). | Two columns: be questions / do questions. |
| -s after does (Does she plays?). | Board: does takes the -s; the verb is base form. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students and create a need for questions. **Interaction:** T → Ss, whole class

1. Write five answers about yourself on the board: *Colombo · 2 · cricket · 6.30 · tea*.
2. Say: *"These are answers about me. What are the questions?"* Let students try. Accept any attempts; don't correct yet.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Part A: Context (8 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To present the target language in context. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the interview with a new student, Ayesha. Match the questions and answers."* (4 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"Do you match or write?"* (Match.)
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class.

**Part B: Language clarification (12 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To clarify form, meaning and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. **be questions.** Board *You are a student.* → *Are you a student?* Draw an arrow showing *are* moving to the front. Elicit the short answers.
2. **do / does questions.** Board *You like cricket.* → *Do you like cricket?* and *She plays the guitar.* → *Does she play the guitar?* Highlight *does* + base verb. Ask the CCQs.
3. **Wh- questions.** Add the question word at the front: *Where are you from? Where does he live?* Build the question-word list on the left.
4. Board the errors *Do you are ...?* and *Does she plays?* and cross them out.
5. Return to the Stage 1 answers. Students now make the correct questions: *Where do you live? How many children do you have?*
6. **Pronunciation.** Model and drill rising and falling intonation: *Do you like cricket? ↗ Where do you live? ↘*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: be or do / does? Exercise B: put the words in order. Six minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Are you a student: be or do?"* (Be.)
2. Pairs check. Students read the questions aloud with the correct intonation.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use questions in a personalised interview. **Interaction:** Pairs, then mingle, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 3. **Write (3 minutes).** Students complete the questions on the interview card.
2. **Interview (5 minutes).** Students interview a partner and write the answers.
3. **Find a friend (6 minutes).** Students mingle and ask the yes / no questions to find someone with the same answers as them: *"Do you have a cat?" "Yes, I do!" "Me too!"*
   - ICQs: *"What do you want to find?"* (Someone with the same answers.)
4. **Report (4 minutes).** Students tell the class one thing they have in common with someone: *"Kasun and I both like cricket."*
5. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four questions from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick round: each student asks the next student one question.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write 8 questions to ask a new classmate (4 with *be*, 4 with *do / does*). Then write your own answers.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students question frames: *Do you ...? · Are you ...? · Where do you ...?*
- In the interview, allow them to read the questions from the card.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students ask a follow-up question each time: *"Do you have a pet?" "Yes." "What's its name?"*
- They use *how often* and *which*: *How often do you play?*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2.
- In the interview, students use *do / does* in present simple questions and no *do* with *be*.
- Students use rising and falling intonation appropriately.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students stop using statement word order?
- Was the "find a friend" activity motivating?
