# Reporting Verbs and Their Patterns (advise, refuse, deny, suggest)

*Lesson theme: Drama at the Team Meeting*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Reported Speech |
| Grammar point | Reporting Verbs and Their Patterns (advise, refuse, deny, suggest) |
| Level | B2 (Upper-intermediate) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Upper-intermediate students who report statements, questions and commands accurately with say, tell and ask, but their reports are repetitive. When they try other verbs, they make pattern errors: He suggested me to go, She denied to take it, He apologised to be late. |
| Main aim | To develop students' use of a range of reporting verbs and their patterns: verb + infinitive, verb + object + infinitive, verb + -ing, verb + preposition + -ing, and verb + that-clause. |
| Subsidiary aims | To develop fluency in summarising a conversation; to raise awareness that reporting verbs show the speaker's purpose and attitude. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can summarise a meeting using at least eight different reporting verbs, e.g. The coach refused to change the team. Malik apologised for missing training. Ayesha suggested holding extra practice. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (transcript of a team meeting), Handout 2 (reference table and practice), Handout 3 (further practice), Handout 4 (role cards for a club meeting). |

## Language analysis

### Patterns

| **Pattern** | **Verbs** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| verb + to + infinitive | agree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten, decide | She refused to change the team. |
| verb + object + to + infinitive | advise, ask, encourage, invite, persuade, remind, tell, warn | He advised me to rest. He warned us not to be late. |
| verb + -ing | admit, deny, recommend, suggest | He denied taking the ball. She suggested practising more. |
| verb + preposition + -ing | apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on | He apologised for missing training. |
| verb + that-clause | admit, agree, complain, explain, promise, suggest (that sb should) | She suggested (that) we should train on Sundays. |

### Meaning and use

- Reporting verbs summarise the **function** of what was said (a promise, a refusal, an apology), not the exact words.
- *"Sorry I'm late."* → *He apologised for being late.* *"I didn't take it!"* → *He denied taking it.*

### Pronunciation

- Stress the reporting verb, which carries the meaning: *She reFUSED to change it. He deNIED taking it.*

### Concept checking questions

*Malik denied taking the ball.*

1. Did Malik say he took the ball? (No, he said he didn't.)
2. Do we know if he took it? (No.)

*The coach advised Ruwan to rest.*

1. Was it an order or advice? (Advice.)
2. Who will rest? (Ruwan.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| suggest + object + infinitive (He suggested me to go). | Board: suggest + -ing / suggest (that) + clause; cross out suggested me to. |
| deny / admit + infinitive (She denied to take). | Group verbs by pattern; card sort. |
| Preposition + infinitive (apologised for to be late). | Rule: after a preposition → -ing. |
| not in the wrong place (warned us to not be late). | Board: warned us not to be late. |
| Too many verbs to remember. | Learn by pattern groups; students choose 10 to learn. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To introduce the context and engage students. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Ask: *"Have you ever been in a team or club meeting where people argued? What did people say?"*
2. Pairs discuss (2 minutes). Take one or two stories.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present reporting verbs in context and clarify their patterns and meanings. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the transcript of the cricket team meeting. What was the problem? How did it end?"* (3 minutes.)
2. Pairs compare. Then students read the coach's summary email and answer the three questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (5 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Match each line in the transcript to a sentence in the email. Underline the reporting verb and the word after it."* Pairs compare.

**Part C: Clarification (9 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 2 (reference table). Elicit the five pattern groups and build them in the centre of the board.
2. Ask the CCQs from the Language analysis.
3. Focus on the most common errors: *suggest*, *deny / admit*, preposition + *-ing*, the position of *not*.
4. Point out that the verb summarises the speaker's purpose: *"Sorry I'm late"* = an apology → *apologise for*.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill with stress on the reporting verb: *She reFUSED to change the team. He deNIED taking it.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the patterns accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Say: *"Do Exercise A on Handout 2: report each sentence using the verb given. Then Exercise B: correct the mistakes."* (7 minutes.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.
3. Fast finishers do Handout 3 (further practice); otherwise set it for homework.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use a range of reporting verbs fluently in summarising a meeting. **Interaction:** Groups of four, then new groups, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 4. Groups of four role-play a meeting of the school drama club about a problem with the end-of-year show. Each student has a role card with things they must say (an apology, a promise, a refusal, a suggestion, a warning, an accusation) (7 minutes).
   - ICQs: *"Do you read your card aloud?"* (No, use your own words.) *"What must you include?"* (The things on your card.)
2. **Report (8 minutes).** Students form new groups and summarise their meeting using at least eight reporting verbs: *"Nethmi apologised for losing the script. Kamal refused to play the king. I suggested postponing the show."*
3. The class votes for the most dramatic meeting (3 minutes).
4. Monitor and note language use.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board sentences from Stage 4 (correct and incorrect). Students correct the errors.
2. Ask: *"Which pattern does suggest take? Which does deny take?"*
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Complete Handout 3. Then write the minutes (summary) of a real or imaginary meeting or family discussion (180 words) using at least ten different reporting verbs.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the reference table during the freer practice.
- Limit them to eight verbs (two from each of four patterns).

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *accuse sb of*, *blame sb for*, *congratulate sb on* and *insist that sb (should)*.
- They use passive and perfect forms: *He denied having been told. She admitted having made a mistake.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 6 out of 8 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- The summary uses at least eight different reporting verbs with the correct pattern.
- No *suggest* + object + infinitive errors in the final summaries.

## Teacher's reflection

- Which patterns caused the most errors?
- Did students choose verbs that matched the speaker's purpose?

Dear parents, At today's meeting, I explained that we had lost three matches in a row. Malik apologised for missing training. I advised Ruwan to rest this weekend. I warned the players not to be late on Sunday, and Kasun offered to bring the drinks.

2. What did Ayesha suggest? ______________________________
3. What did the coach refuse to do? ______________________________
