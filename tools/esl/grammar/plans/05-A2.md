GRAMMAR TOPIC: ARTICLES · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# A, An or The? (First and Second Mention, Unique Things)

*Lesson theme: The Dog in the Market*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Articles |
| Grammar point | A, An or The? (First and Second Mention, Unique Things) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who use a / an correctly but don't use the, or use the everywhere. Their stories are confusing: I saw dog. Dog took fish. |
| Main aim | To clarify and practise a / an for first mention and the for second mention, unique things and things both speakers know. |
| Subsidiary aims | To develop fluency in telling a short picture story; to give and follow directions in a town. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can tell a short story and give directions using a / an and the correctly. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (story: The Dog in the Market), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (town map and directions task), story picture prompts (drawn on the board or described). |

## Language analysis

### Meaning and use

| **Use** | **Article** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| First mention (the listener doesn't know which one) | a / an | I saw a dog in the market. |
| Second mention (now the listener knows which one) | the | The dog took a fish. |
| Unique things (there is only one) | the | the sun, the moon, the sea, the sky, the internet |
| Shared knowledge (both speakers know which one) | the | Can you close the door? (the door in this room) Let's meet at the station. (our town's station) |
| Superlatives | the | the biggest fish |

### Pronunciation

- *the* /ðə/ before consonant sounds: *the dog*; /ði/ before vowel sounds: *the apple, the end*.

### Concept checking questions

*I saw a dog. The dog was huge.*

1. In the first sentence, does the listener know which dog? (No.)
2. In the second sentence, do we know which dog? (Yes, the dog I just talked about.)

*The sun is hot.*

1. How many suns are there? (One.) So *a* or *the*? (The.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| No article at all (Sun is hot). | Board the "only one" rule with pictures. |
| the for first mention (I bought the new phone = any phone). | Ask: "Does your friend know which phone?" |
| Pronunciation of the before vowels. | Drill the apple, the old man. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To create interest with a funny picture story. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Draw or describe six simple pictures on the board: a market; a dog; a fish stall; the dog taking a fish; a man chasing the dog; the fish landing in a woman's basket.
2. Ask: *"What's happening? Is it funny?"* Take ideas.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present *a / an* and *the* in a story and clarify first and second mention, unique things and shared knowledge. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the story. Is it the same as the pictures? Three minutes."*
2. Students answer the three questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Circle every a / an. Underline every the."* Pairs compare.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. **First and second mention.** Board: *I saw a dog. The dog was huge.* Ask the CCQs from the Language analysis. Build the rule.
2. **Unique things.** Elicit *the sun* from the text. Ask: *"How many suns are there?"* Add *the moon, the sea, the sky, the internet.*
3. **Shared knowledge.** Ask: *"The market: which market? Do the people in the story know?"* (Yes, their local market.) Add *Close the door. Meet me at the station.*
4. **Superlatives.** Elicit *the best day* from the text.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill *the dog, the apple, the end, the old man.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise article choice. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: a, an or the. Exercise B: find and correct seven mistakes. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs explain each choice: *"First time, so a."*
3. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use articles accurately in storytelling and giving directions. **Interaction:** Pairs

1. **Story (6 minutes).** Pairs retell the dog story without looking, then invent a new ending.
2. **Directions (9 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. Student A chooses a place on the map and gives directions from the bus station: *"Go past the bank. There's a café next to the park."* Student B follows and guesses the place. Swap.
   - ICQs: *"Where do you start?"* (The bus station.) *"Do you say the name of the place?"* (No, your partner guesses.)
3. **Writing (3 minutes).** Students write a 4-sentence mini-story at the bottom of Handout 3 using first and second mention.
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Two students read their mini-stories. The class checks each article.
2. Board errors from Stage 4; students correct them.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write a funny 6-sentence story about an animal, using first and second mention correctly.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give a decision flowchart: *Is it the first time? → a / an. Do we know which one? → the.*
- Provide the first sentence of each story.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students explain each article choice to their partner.
- Add superlatives to the directions: *the biggest temple, the oldest shop*.

## Assessment and success criteria

- Students change from *a* to *the* on second mention in their stories.
- 12 out of 16 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Correct *the* with unique and shared-knowledge nouns.

## Teacher's reflection

- Were unique and shared-knowledge uses clear?
- Did the directions task produce accurate article use?

# Handout 1: The Dog in the Market

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Read the story. Circle a / an. Underline the.**

Last Saturday, I went to the market with my grandmother. The sun was very hot and the market was busy. There was a man with a bicycle, a woman with a big basket and an old man selling fruit.

Suddenly, I saw a dog. The dog was huge and very hungry. It walked to a fish stall and took a big fish! The man with the bicycle shouted, "Stop!" and chased the dog. The dog ran under a table and the fish flew into the air. Where did it land? In the woman's basket!

The woman laughed and gave the fish back to the fish seller. The dog didn't get a fish, but my grandmother bought it a bun. It was the best day at the market ever!

# Answer Key

**Handout 1**

1. Her / his grandmother · 2. A big fish · 3. In the woman's basket

**Handout 2, Exercise A**

1. a ... The · 2. The · 3. an · 4. the · 5. an ... The ... the · 6. an ... the · 7. a ... a ... The · 8. – (no article) ... the ... the

**Handout 2, Exercise B**

Yesterday I saw a film at **the** cinema. **The** film was about a girl and a horse. **The** girl lived on **a** farm near **the** sea. One day, **the** horse ran away. It was **the** saddest day of her life.

**Common errors to watch for**

| **Error** | **Correction** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Sun is hot. | The sun is hot. |
| I saw a film. A film was great. | I saw a film. The film was great. |
| Please open a door. (there is only one door) | Please open the door. |
| It was a best day. | It was the best day. |
