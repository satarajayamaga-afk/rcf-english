GRAMMAR TOPIC: PREPOSITIONS · A1 (Beginner) · 60 minutes

# Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between)

*Lesson theme: Where Is It? My Bedroom*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Prepositions |
| Grammar point | Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Beginners who know There is / There are and common furniture and classroom words, but confuse in and on and leave out to in next to. |
| Main aim | To present and practise seven prepositions of place to describe where things are. |
| Subsidiary aims | To review furniture vocabulary; to practise Where is / Where are ...? questions; to develop listening and speaking in a picture dictation. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can describe where 8 things are in a room and draw a room from a partner's description. |
| Materials | A box and a ball (or a cup and a pen), Handout 1 (Kamal's bedroom description), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (picture dictation grid), classroom objects. |

## Language analysis

### Form and meaning

| **Preposition** | **Meaning** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| in | inside | The ball is in the box. |
| on | on top of, touching a surface | The book is on the table. |
| under | below | The cat is under the bed. |
| next to | at the side of | The lamp is next to the bed. |
| behind | at the back of | The bag is behind the door. |
| in front of | facing, before | The chair is in front of the desk. |
| between | in the middle of two things | The desk is between the bed and the window. |

Structures: *Where is the ...? It's on the ... · Where are the ...? They're under the ... · There's a ... next to the ...*

### Pronunciation

- Weak *of*: *in front of* /ɪn ˈfrʌnt əv/. Weak *the*: /ðə/.
- *next to* – the /t/ sounds often join: /ˈneks tə/.

### Concept checking questions

*The ball is in the box.* — Can I see all of the ball? (No, it's inside.) *The ball is on the box.* — Is it inside? (No.) Is it touching the top? (Yes.) *The desk is between the bed and the window.* — How many things are on the sides? (Two.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| in / on confusion (in the table). | Real objects; repeat with the box many times. |
| Missing to (next the window). | Board next to; gesture. |
| between with one object. | Always show two objects. |
| Where is my shoes? | Contrast is / are with singular / plural. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To introduce the topic with real objects. **Interaction:** T → Ss

1. Hold up a box and a ball. Put the ball in the box. Ask: *"Where's the ball?"* Students point. Say: *"It's in the box."*
2. Move the ball: on, under, next to, behind, in front of the box. Let students guess the words.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Part A: Context (8 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To present the language in context. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read about Kamal's bedroom. Where are his things? Answer the questions."* (4 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"Do you draw or write?"* (Write.)
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class.

**Part B: Language clarification (12 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Draw seven box-and-ball pictures on the board. Elicit each preposition from the text and write it under the picture.
2. Ask the CCQs.
3. Highlight *next* ***to***, *in front* ***of***, *between ...* ***and*** *...*
4. Questions: board *Where is the lamp? Where are the shoes?* Contrast *is / are*.
5. Drill: *Where's the cat? It's under the bed. Where are the books? They're on the shelf.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. Students complete Handout 2, Exercises A and B (6 minutes).
2. Pairs check. Feedback.
3. **Classroom drill:** ask about real objects in the classroom: *"Where's my bag?" "It's on the desk."*

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the prepositions in communicative tasks. **Interaction:** Pairs

1. **Hide and find (6 minutes):** Student A hides a small object (an eraser) while B closes their eyes. B asks: *"Is it under the chair?" "No, it isn't." "Is it in your bag?" "Yes, it is!"* Swap.
2. **Picture dictation (10 minutes):** give out Handout 3. Student A draws their dream bedroom in their grid (8 things) without showing B. A describes it; B draws it. Then compare drawings and swap.
3. Students write 4 sentences about their partner's drawing.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Compare a few drawings. Which pairs have the most similar pictures?
2. Correct errors heard (*in the table, next the window*).
3. Set homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Draw your bedroom and write 6 sentences about where things are.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Label the box-and-ball pictures and leave them on the board.
- Weaker students describe 5 things instead of 8.

**Stretch**

- Add *opposite, above, near, on the left / right of*.
- Students describe the classroom from memory with eyes closed.

## Assessment and success criteria

- Students use the correct preposition in 8 of 10 items on Handout 2.
- In the picture dictation, the partner's drawing matches in at least 6 of 8 positions.

## Teacher's reflection

- Which prepositions caused most confusion?
- Did the picture dictation work well?
