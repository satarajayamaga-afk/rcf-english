GRAMMAR TOPIC: ARTICLES · A1 (Beginner) · 60 minutes

# A and An

*Lesson theme: What's in My Bag? Jobs and Things*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Articles |
| Grammar point | A and An |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Beginners who know some classroom objects and jobs but often leave out the article (She is teacher) or choose a / an by spelling, not sound. |
| Main aim | To present and practise a and an with singular countable nouns, for objects and jobs. |
| Subsidiary aims | To review classroom and personal objects; to practise vowel and consonant sounds at the start of words; to develop speaking through a guessing game. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can describe 8 objects in their bag and say people's jobs using a / an correctly. |
| Materials | A bag of real objects (a pen, an apple, an umbrella, a book, an orange, an eraser, a phone, an ID card), Handout 1 (What's in my bag? script), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (jobs guessing game cards). |

## Language analysis

### Form

|  | **Rule** | **Examples** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| a | before a consonant sound | a book, a pen, a car, a doctor, a uniform, a university, a one-day trip |
| an | before a vowel sound | an apple, an egg, an orange, an engineer, an hour, an honest man, an MP3 |
| no article | plural nouns | I've got two pens. They're doctors. |

Note: It's the **sound** that matters, not the letter: *a uniform* /ə ˈjuːnɪfɔːm/ (starts with a /j/ sound); *an hour* /ən ˈaʊə/ (the *h* is silent).

### Meaning and use

- *a / an* = one, any one (not a specific one): *I've got a pen.*
- Jobs always need *a / an*: *She's a nurse. He's an engineer.* (not *She's nurse*)

### Pronunciation

- *a* is weak: /ə/. *an* is weak: /ən/. They link to the next word: *an apple* /ə‿ˈnæpl/.

### Concept checking questions

*an apple*

1. Is *apple* one thing or many? (One.)
2. What sound does *apple* start with? (A vowel sound, /æ/.)

*a uniform*

1. Does *uniform* start with the letter u? (Yes.) What sound? (/j/, like *you*.) So *a* or *an*? (A.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing article with jobs (She is teacher). | Board: She is a teacher. Use a finger gesture for the missing word. |
| Choosing by letter (an university). | Say the words aloud; ask "What sound?" |
| a + plural (a two books, a books). | Contrast one vs many with real objects. |
| Not linking an + vowel. | Drill an‿apple, an‿egg. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students and activate vocabulary. **Interaction:** T → Ss, whole class

1. Show the bag. Say: *"What's in my bag? Guess!"* Take a few guesses.
2. Pull objects out one at a time. Students name them. Board the nouns without articles first.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Part A: Context (8 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To present the language in context. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read about Ishara's bag. Tick the things in her bag."* (3 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"Do you tick or write?"* (Tick.)
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class.
3. Students answer two questions: *What is Ishara's mother's job? What is her father's job?*

**Part B: Language clarification (12 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Point to the nouns on the board. Ask students to tell you *a* or *an* for each from the text. Write *a* or *an* in front.
2. Sort into two columns: *a* and *an*. Ask: *"What's the same about the an words?"* Elicit: they start with a vowel sound.
3. **The sound rule.** Write *university* and *umbrella*. Say them slowly. Ask: *"Same first sound?"* (No.) Write *a university, an umbrella.* Do the same with *hour* and *house*.
4. **Jobs.** Board *She's a doctor. He's an engineer.* Ask: *"Can I say She's doctor?"* (No.)
5. **Plurals.** Hold up two pens: *"Two pens. A two pens?"* (No.)
6. **Pronunciation.** Drill weak forms and linking: *a pen, an apple, an engineer, an hour.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the rule accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs

1. Students complete Handout 2, Exercises A and B (6 minutes).
2. Pairs check. Nominate students to read answers aloud, linking *an* + vowel.
3. **Quick-fire game:** say a noun; students shout *"A!"* or *"An!"*. Include tricky ones: *hour, uniform, egg, umbrella, one-day trip*.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use *a / an* in personalised speaking. **Interaction:** Pairs, groups

1. **What's in my bag? (6 minutes):** students draw five things from their own bag in a box on Handout 2 (without words), then describe them to a partner, who draws them: *"I've got a phone, an eraser and a red pen."*
2. **Jobs guessing game (8 minutes):** in groups, students take a card from Handout 3 and mime the job. Others ask: *"Are you a doctor?" "No, I'm not." "Are you an artist?" "Yes, I am!"*
3. **Writing (4 minutes):** students write 6 sentences about their bag and their family's jobs.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences heard (two incorrect). Students correct them.
2. Final quick-fire round with tricky words.
3. Set homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write 8 sentences about your bag and your family's jobs.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Highlight vowel letters on the noun cards in a colour.
- Give weaker students a list of jobs with *a / an* already written.

**Stretch**

- Add adjectives: *an old book, a big apple, an expensive phone*. Ask: *"Does the article change?"*
- Introduce tricky sounds: *an MP3, a European city, an honest boy*.

## Assessment and success criteria

- Students use *a / an* correctly in 8 of 10 items on Handout 2.
- Students use *a / an* with jobs in the guessing game.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students understand the sound rule, not the letter rule?
- Did they remember the article with jobs?
