*Lesson theme: Writing a Travel Guide*

## Language analysis

### Prepositions in relative clauses

| **Register** | **Pattern** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Informal (most common) | preposition at the end; pronoun often left out | the hotel (that) we stayed in · the friend (who) I went with |
| Formal | preposition + which / whom | the hotel in which we stayed · the man to whom I spoke |
| Not possible | preposition + that / who | the hotel in that ✗ · the man to who ✗ |
| Quantity (formal) | some / many / most / all / none of whom / which | 200 people came, most of whom were tourists. |

### Sentence relatives

- *which* can refer to a **whole clause**, often to comment on it: *The train was late,* ***which*** *meant we missed the boat.* (not *what*)

### Reduced relative clauses

- **Active** → *-ing*: *the road* ***that leads*** *to the fort* → *the road* ***leading*** *to the fort*.
- **Passive** → past participle: *a temple* ***which was built*** *in 1592* → *a temple* ***built*** *in 1592*.
- Common in guides, news and academic writing because they are concise.

### Pronunciation

- Sentence relatives have a pause before *which* and a falling intonation: *We missed the boat, | which was a shame ↘.*

### Concept checking questions

*The hotel in which we stayed was by the sea.*

1. Did we stay in the hotel? (Yes.)
2. Is this formal or informal? (Formal.) What's the informal version? (*the hotel we stayed in*.)

*The train was late, which meant we missed the boat.*

1. What does *which* refer to: the train or the fact that it was late? (The fact that it was late.)

*a temple built in 1592*

1. Did the temple build something? (No, it was built.) Active or passive meaning? (Passive.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing preposition (the hotel which we stayed). | Ask: "stay ___ a hotel?" Put the preposition back. |
| to who / in that | Board: after a preposition → whom / which only. |
| what for sentence relatives (..., what annoyed me). | Board which in colour; cross out what. |
| Wrong participle (a temple building in 1592). | Active or passive? Test with which was / which is. |
| Over-formal speech. | Discuss register: use end prepositions when speaking. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To introduce the topic and engage students. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Ask: *"Imagine a tourist asks you for the best place to visit in your town. What would you recommend? Why?"*
2. Pairs discuss (2 minutes). Take two or three recommendations.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present the structures in context and clarify their form, meaning and register. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the travel guide entry about Galle Fort. Would you like to visit? Find three things to do. Three minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the four questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Find: two relative clauses with a preposition, one sentence where which refers to a whole idea, and three -ing or -ed phrases that describe a noun."* Pairs compare.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. **Prepositions.** Board *the hotel in which we stayed* and *the hotel we stayed in.* Ask the CCQs. Discuss register. Board *in that* ✗ and *to who* ✗.
2. **Quantity.** Board *most of whom were tourists*.
3. **Sentence relatives.** Board *The train was late, which meant we missed the boat.* Ask the CCQs. Board *what* ✗.
4. **Reduced clauses.** Expand *the road leading to the fort* → *the road that leads* and *a temple built in 1592* → *which was built*. Ask the CCQs.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill the pause and fall: *We missed the boat, | which was a shame.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the structures accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Read the reference box. Exercise A: rewrite in a formal style. Exercise B: reduce the relative clauses. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.
3. Fast finishers do Handout 3 (further practice); otherwise set it for homework.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the structures accurately in a piece of descriptive writing. **Interaction:** Pairs, then groups, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 4. Pairs choose one place and write a travel guide entry (about 150 words). They must use at least two prepositions in relative clauses, one sentence relative and three reduced relative clauses (11 minutes).
   - ICQs: *"How many reduced clauses?"* (At least three.) *"Formal or informal style?"* (Quite formal: it's a guide.)
2. Pairs swap with another pair and underline each structure. They suggest one improvement (4 minutes).
3. Two pairs read their entries aloud. The class votes for the place they most want to visit (3 minutes).
4. Monitor and note language use.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board sentences from Stage 4 (correct and incorrect). Students correct the errors.
2. Ask: *"How would you say 'the hotel in which we stayed' to a friend?"*
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Complete Handout 3. Then write a travel guide entry (180 words) for a place in your town, using all the structures from today.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the reference table during the writing.
- Reduce the requirement to one of each structure.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *whereby*, *during which* and *by which time*.
- They use reduced clauses with *having + pp*: *Visitors, having explored the ramparts, can relax in a café.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- The travel guide entry includes all three structures used accurately.
- No *in that / to who* or *..., what ...* errors.
- 6 out of 8 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students choose the right register for prepositions?
- Did the reduced clauses make the writing more concise?

# Handout 1: Travel Guide Extract

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**GALLE FORT: Where History Meets the Sea**

Galle Fort, built by the Portuguese in 1588 and later strengthened by the Dutch, is the town in which most visitors to the south coast spend at least a day. The narrow streets, lined with colonial houses, are full of cafés, boutiques and galleries.

Start your visit on the ramparts, from which you can see the whole bay. Visitors arriving early in the morning will see local fishermen at work, which is an unforgettable sight. The lighthouse, standing at the southern tip of the fort, is the most photographed building in Galle.

The Fort is home to around 400 families, most of whom have lived there for generations. Ask any of the shopkeepers you talk to, and they'll tell you stories about the history of their houses.

In the evening, walk along the walls as the sun goes down, which is the best time of day to be there.

# Handout 2: Language Focus and Practice

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Language reference**

| **Structure** | **Informal** | **Formal / concise** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Preposition | the town we stayed in | the town in which we stayed |
| Preposition + person | the man I spoke to | the man to whom I spoke |
| Quantity | ... and most of them were tourists | ..., most of whom were tourists |
| Sentence relative | The bus was late. That meant we missed it. | The bus was late, which meant we missed it. |
| Reduced (active) | the road that leads to the fort | the road leading to the fort |
| Reduced (passive) | a temple which was built in 1592 | a temple built in 1592 |

**A. Rewrite in a formal style.**

1. This is the hotel we stayed in. → This is the hotel ______________________________
2. She's the woman I gave the money to. → She's the woman ______________________________
3. That's the company my father works for. → That's the company ______________________________
4. The guide had 30 tourists, and most of them were from Germany. → The guide had 30 tourists, ______________________________
5. The beach we swam at was beautiful. → The beach ______________________________
6. He apologised. That surprised everyone. → He apologised, ______________________________
7. There are four temples in the town. Two of them are very old. → There are four temples in the town, ______________________________
8. Is this the topic you're interested in? → Is this the topic ______________________________

**B. Reduce the relative clauses.**

1. The bus that goes to Kandy leaves at 6. → ______________________________
2. The paintings which were found in the cave are 1,500 years old. → ______________________________
