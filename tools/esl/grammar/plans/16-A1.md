# Everyday Phrasal Verbs for Daily Routines (get up, put on, turn off)

*Lesson theme: My Morning and Evening*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Phrasal Verbs |
| Grammar point | Everyday Phrasal Verbs for Daily Routines (get up, put on, turn off) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Beginners who use the present simple for routines (I go to school at 7). They know single verbs but not phrasal verbs: they say I wake at 6 or I open the light, and leave out the particle (I get at 6 o'clock). |
| Main aim | To present and practise ten everyday phrasal verbs for routines (wake up, get up, put on, take off, turn on, turn off, sit down, stand up, go out, come back) in the present simple. |
| Subsidiary aims | To practise third person -s on the verb, not the particle (She gets up); to practise stress on the particle; to develop speaking through a "my day" mime and interview. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can describe their daily routine with phrasal verbs, e.g. I wake up at 6. I get up at 6.15. I put on my uniform. I turn off the TV at 9. |
| Materials | Picture cards of the ten actions, Handout 1 (Sahan's day), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (interview). |

## Language analysis

### Form and meaning

| **Phrasal verb** | **Meaning** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| wake up | stop sleeping | I wake up at 6. |
| get up | get out of bed | I get up at 6.15. |
| put on | start wearing (clothes) | I put on my uniform. |
| take off | stop wearing (clothes) | I take off my shoes. |
| turn on | start a machine or light | I turn on the TV. |
| turn off | stop a machine or light | I turn off the light. |
| sit down | go from standing to sitting | Please sit down. |
| stand up | go from sitting to standing | Stand up, please. |
| go out | leave the house (for fun) | I go out with my friends. |
| come back | return | I come back home at 4. |

- A phrasal verb = **verb + small word (particle)**: *get + up*. Together they have one meaning.
- In the present simple, add *-s* to the **verb**, not the particle: *She gets up* (not *She get ups*).
- Negatives and questions: *I don't get up early. What time do you get up?*

### Pronunciation

- The particle is stressed: *get UP, turn OFF, sit DOWN*.
- Linking: *get up* /ge tʌp/, *put on* /pʊ tɒn/, *turn off* /tɜː nɒf/.

### Concept checking questions

*I turn off the light.*

1. Is the light on or off after this? (Off.)

*She gets up at 7.*

1. Is she in bed after this? (No.) Where is the *-s*? (On *get*.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing particle (I get at 6). | Mime and stress the particle; board the verbs in two colours. |
| Wrong verb (open / close the light). | Teach turn on / turn off with a real light switch. |
| -s on the particle (get ups). | Board: She gets up ✓ / get ups ✗. |
| Confusing wake up and get up. | Mime: eyes open (wake up), then out of bed (get up). |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students and introduce the topic. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Mime your morning: yawn and stretch (wake up), get out of a chair (get up), put on a jacket. Ask: *"What am I doing?"* Accept any words.
2. Ask: *"What time do you go to school?"* Take a few answers.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present the ten phrasal verbs and clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class, individual, pairs

**Part A: Meaning (8 minutes)**

1. Show each picture card and mime the action. Say the phrasal verb; students repeat. Stick the cards on the board.
2. **Simon says.** Give quick commands: *"Stand up! Sit down! Put on your bag! Take off your bag!"* Students do the actions.

**Part B: Context (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read about Sahan's day. What time does he get up? Three minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Do you read or write first?"* (Read.)
2. Students number the pictures in order. Pairs compare. Check as a class.

**Part C: Clarification (6 minutes)**

1. Board *I get up at 6.15.* and *Sahan gets up at 6.15.* Ask the CCQs. Circle the *-s* on *gets*.
2. Board the negative and question: *I don't get up early. What time do you get up?*
3. Board *I open the light* ✗ → *I turn on the light*.
4. **Pronunciation.** Drill with stress on the particle and linking: *get UP, turn OFF, put ON*.

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the phrasal verbs accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: write the missing word (up, on, off, down, out, back). Exercise B: write the opposite. Six minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Turn ___ the TV. Which word?"* (On / off.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the phrasal verbs to talk about real routines. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. **My day (5 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. Students complete the "Me" column with their times and actions.
2. **Interview (7 minutes).** Students interview a partner: *"What time do you wake up?" "At 5.30." "Do you turn on the TV in the morning?"* They write the answers.
   - ICQs: *"Do you write your answers or your partner's?"* (Both, in different columns.)
3. **Mime game (6 minutes).** In groups, one student mimes part of their routine; the others guess: *"You put on your shoes!"* Then two students tell the class about their partner: *"Nimal gets up at 5.30. He goes out on Saturday."*
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick "Simon says" round with all ten verbs.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write about the day of someone in your family (8 sentences). Use 6 phrasal verbs from today: *My mother wakes up at 5. She ...*

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the picture cards with the words to keep on the desk.
- In the interview, give them the question frame *What time do you ...?*

**Stretch**

- Stronger students learn four more: *lie down, look for, put away, hang up* (clothes).
- They write questions about another person: *What time does your father get up?*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 12 out of 14 or better on Handout 2.
- In the interview, students use the particle every time and *-s* on the verb for *he / she*.
- Students use *turn on / turn off* for lights and machines.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did the mime and "Simon says" help students remember the particles?
- Did students use *-s* in the right place when reporting?

# Handout 1: Sahan's Day

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

Sahan is 12. He lives in Kurunegala.

Sahan **wakes up** at 6 o'clock, but he **gets up** at 6.15. He **puts on** his school uniform and has breakfast. He **goes out** at 7 and walks to school.

At school, the students **stand up** when the teacher comes in. Then they **sit down**.

Sahan **comes back** home at 2.30. He **takes off** his shoes and **turns on** the TV. He does his homework at 4. At 9.30, he **turns off** the light and goes to sleep.
