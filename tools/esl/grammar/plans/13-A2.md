GRAMMAR TOPIC: GERUNDS AND INFINITIVES · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# Verb + -ing or Verb + To (enjoy, finish, decide, hope) and the Infinitive of Purpose

*Lesson theme: Summer Plans*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Gerunds and Infinitives |
| Grammar point | Verb + -ing or Verb + To (enjoy, finish, decide, hope) and the Infinitive of Purpose |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who use like / love + -ing and want / would like + to. They use the wrong pattern with other common verbs (I enjoy to swim, I decided going), and they explain reasons with for (I went to the shop for buy milk). |
| Main aim | To clarify and practise common verbs followed by -ing (enjoy, finish, mind, stop, can't stand, practise) and by to + infinitive (decide, hope, plan, learn, need, promise, forget), and the infinitive of purpose (to + verb = why). |
| Subsidiary aims | To read an email for specific information; to develop speaking about plans and reasons. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can talk about their summer plans and give reasons, e.g. I've decided to learn to swim. I'm going to Galle to visit my cousins. I hope to finish reading my book. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (email from Ruwan), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (verb sorting cards), Handout 4 (summer plans planner). |

## Language analysis

### Form

| **verb + -ing** | **verb + to + infinitive** |
| :-- | :-- |
| enjoy, finish, mind, stop, practise, can't stand, keep, imagine | decide, hope, plan, learn, need, promise, forget, agree, offer, refuse |
| I enjoy swimming. Have you finished packing? | I've decided to learn French. I hope to see you soon. |

### Infinitive of purpose

- **to + verb** answers *Why?*: *I'm going to Galle* ***to visit*** *my cousins.* (not *for visit* / *for to visit*)
- Negative purpose: *so that ... not / in order not to* (B1 preview only).

### Meaning and use

- There is no simple rule for which verbs take *-ing* and which take *to*; students learn them in groups.
- Many *to*-verbs are about the future (*decide, hope, plan, promise*); many *-ing* verbs are about activities in progress or completed (*enjoy, finish, stop*).

### Pronunciation

- Weak *to* /tə/: *decided to learn* /dɪˈsaɪdɪd tə lɜːn/.

### Concept checking questions

*I've finished packing.*

1. Is the packing complete? (Yes.)

*I'm going to Galle to visit my cousins.*

1. Why am I going to Galle? (To visit my cousins.)
2. Can I say *for visit*? (No.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Wrong pattern (I enjoy to swim, I decided going). | Two-column sort; card game for memory. |
| for + verb for purpose (for buy). | Board to buy; cross out for. |
| Confusing stop doing with stop to do. | Teach only stop + -ing at this level. |
| Long lists overwhelm students. | Focus on 12 high-frequency verbs. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To personalise the topic. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Ask: *"What are you going to do in the next school holidays?"* Pairs share ideas (2 minutes).
2. Take a few ideas; board one sentence exactly as a student says it.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present verb patterns and the infinitive of purpose in context and clarify meaning and form. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read Ruwan's email. What has he decided to do this summer? Three minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the four questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Underline every verb followed by another verb. Is the second verb -ing or to + verb?"* Pairs sort them into two columns.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. Build the two columns on the board from the email. Add more verbs.
2. Point out the "future" feeling of many *to*-verbs (*decide, hope, plan*).
3. **Infinitive of purpose.** Board *I'm going to Galle to visit my cousins.* Ask the CCQs. Board *for visit* ✗.
4. Return to the student's sentence from Stage 1 and check it together.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill: *I've decided to learn. I enjoy swimming. I went there to see my friends.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the patterns accurately. **Interaction:** Pairs, then individual, then whole class

1. **Verb sort (3 minutes).** Pairs sort the verb cards (Handout 3) into *-ing* and *to* piles as fast as possible.
2. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: -ing or to? Exercise B: match and write the reason. Five minutes."*
3. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the patterns in personalised speaking about plans and reasons. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 4. Students complete their summer plans with the prompts (5 minutes): *This summer I've decided to ... · I hope to ... · I need to ... · I'm going to ... to ...*
   - ICQs: *"Real plans or invented?"* (Either.)
2. **Interview (8 minutes).** Students interview two partners and ask why: *"What have you decided to do?" "Why are you going to Kandy?" "To visit my grandmother."*
3. Two or three students tell the class about a partner's plans (3 minutes).
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from Stage 4 (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick quiz: say a verb; students say *-ing* or *to*.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write an email (100 words) to a friend about your holiday plans. Use six verbs from today's lesson and two infinitives of purpose.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the two-column list on a card.
- Focus on six verbs (three from each column).

**Stretch**

- Stronger students add more verbs: *avoid, suggest, consider* (+ -ing); *manage, afford, seem* (+ to).
- They use *in order to* and *so that* for purpose.

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Students use *to* + verb (not *for* + verb) for purpose.
- In the interview, students use the correct pattern with most verbs.

## Teacher's reflection

- Which verbs caused the most confusion?
- Did the verb sort help memory?

# Handout 1: Ruwan's Email

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

Hi Dilan,

School has finished, and I've finally finished studying for my exams! Now I can enjoy relaxing for a few weeks.

I've decided to learn to swim properly this summer. I'm going to the pool every morning to practise. My coach says I need to practise breathing, and I keep forgetting!

In August, I'm planning to go to Jaffna to visit my grandparents. I hope to see the Nallur festival. My grandmother has promised to teach me how to make crab curry. I can't stand waiting, so I'm counting the days!

Do you want to come swimming with me next week? I don't mind waiting for you after your tuition class.

Ruwan
