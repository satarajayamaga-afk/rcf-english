GRAMMAR TOPIC: PREPOSITIONS · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# Prepositions of Time (in, on, at)

*Lesson theme: The School Calendar*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Prepositions |
| Grammar point | Prepositions of Time (in, on, at) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who know days, months and times but mix in, on, at (in Monday, at June, on next week). |
| Main aim | To clarify and practise in, on, at with times, days, dates, months, years and parts of the day, and expressions with no preposition. |
| Subsidiary aims | To develop speaking through a mingle activity; to read a school calendar for specific information. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can ask and answer questions about birthdays, routines and events using in, on, at correctly. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (school calendar), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (Find someone who ...), time expression cards. |

## Language analysis

### Form and use

| **at (small, exact)** | **on (days)** | **in (longer periods)** | **No preposition** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| at 7 o'clock, at 6.30 | on Monday | in June | next week |
| at night | on 4 February | in 2025 | last year |
| at the weekend (UK) | on my birthday | in summer / the dry season | tomorrow |
| at Christmas / at Vesak | on Monday morning | in the morning / afternoon / evening | this morning |
| at lunchtime, at noon | on New Year's Day | in the 1990s | every day |

### Pronunciation

- Weak *at* /ət/: *at seven* /ət ˈsevn/. Linking: *on‿Monday*, *in‿April*.

### Concept checking questions

*in the morning / at night* — Which one uses *at*? (night) Is *night* an exception? (Yes, learn it.) *on Monday morning* — Why *on*, not *in*? (Because it's a specific day.) *next week* — Is there a preposition? (No.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| on next week, in last year | Highlight the "no preposition" column. |
| in the night vs at night | Teach at night as a fixed phrase. |
| on Monday morning vs in the morning | Rule: a day makes it on. |
| Dates: in 5 March | Point to the calendar: a date is a day → on. |

## Board plan

| **Left: triangle** | **Centre: examples** | **Right: no preposition** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| IN (big): months, years, seasons, parts of the day · ON (medium): days, dates · AT (small): times, night, the weekend, festivals | at 7 o'clock · on Monday · on 4 February · in June · in 2025 · in the morning · at night · on Monday morning | next week · last year · tomorrow · this morning · every day |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To create personal interest. **Interaction:** Whole class, pairs

1. Write your birthday on the board: *4 February*. Tell students it's your birthday.
2. Ask: *"When's your birthday? When do you get up? When do you have lunch?"* Students ask a partner (2 minutes).

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present *in, on, at* in context and clarify their use with times, days, dates, months, years and parts of the day. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the school calendar. When is sports day? When do classes finish on Fridays? Answer the four questions."*
   - ICQs: *"How many questions?"* (Four.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Look at the words in bold: in, on, at. What comes after each one?"* Pairs list the time expressions under *in, on, at*.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. Draw the triangle on the board: *in* (big) at the top, *on* (medium), *at* (small). Pairs sort the time expression cards into the triangle. Check and build the board.
2. Ask the CCQs from the Language analysis.
3. Highlight the "no preposition" column: *next week, last year, tomorrow, this morning, every day.*
4. Highlight special cases: *at night* (but *in the morning*), *on Monday morning*.
5. Drill: *at seven, on Monday, in June, at night, next week.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: in, on, at or nothing. Exercise B: sort the expressions. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use prepositions of time accurately in personalised speaking. **Interaction:** Mingle, then pairs

1. Give out Handout 3. **Part A (6 minutes):** students mingle and ask the questions. They write a name for each question.
2. **Part B (6 minutes):** students mingle again with the new questions and must answer in full sentences: *"My birthday is on 12 August."*
   - ICQs: *"In Part B, do you answer with one word or a full sentence?"* (A full sentence.)
3. **Holidays quiz (6 minutes):** pairs write five questions about festivals and holidays (*When is Vesak? When do people celebrate Avurudu?*) and swap with another pair.
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board errors heard in Stage 4; students correct them.
2. Quick test: say a time expression; students shout *in, on, at* or *nothing*.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write your weekly timetable in 8 sentences using *in, on, at* and two expressions with no preposition.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Keep the triangle visible; give a card version.
- Weaker students answer with short phrases in Part A.

**Stretch**

- Add *in the 1990s, at the end of, in the middle of*.
- Write a weekly timetable with 10 time expressions.

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Students answer accurately in full sentences in Part B.

## Teacher's reflection

- Which expressions were hardest?
- Did students answer more accurately in Part B than in Part A?

**Common errors to watch for**

| **Error** | **Correction** |
| :-- | :-- |
| I'll see you on next week. | I'll see you next week. |
| My birthday is in 5 March. | My birthday is on 5 March. |
| I get up in 6 o'clock. | I get up at 6 o'clock. |
| I study in the night. | I study at night. |
