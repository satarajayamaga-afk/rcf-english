GRAMMAR TOPIC: CONDITIONALS · A1 (Beginner) · 60 minutes

# Zero Conditional (If + present, present)

*Lesson theme: Facts About Our World*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Conditionals |
| Grammar point | Zero Conditional (If + present, present) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | High beginners who use the present simple for facts and routines (Water boils at 100 degrees. I drink tea in the morning.) but cannot yet join two ideas with if. They say If I tired, I sleep. |
| Main aim | To present and practise the zero conditional (If + present simple, present simple) for facts and routines. |
| Subsidiary aims | To review the present simple, including he/she/it -s; to develop speaking through a matching game and a partner interview. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can say and write 6 true sentences with If ..., ... about science facts and their own routines. |
| Materials | A glass of water, an ice cube and a plant (or pictures), Handout 1 (science facts), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (half-sentence cards), Handout 4 (my routines). |

## Language analysis

### Form

| **If clause** | **Main clause** |
| :-- | :-- |
| If + present simple | present simple |
| If you heat ice, | it melts. |
| If I am tired, | I go to bed early. |
| If it rains, | we don't play outside. |

- The two clauses can change places: *Ice melts* ***if*** *you heat it.* We use a comma only when *If* comes first.
- *If* can be replaced by *When* with the same meaning: *When I'm hungry, I eat fruit.*

### Meaning and use

- Facts that are always true: *If you mix red and yellow, you get orange.*
- Routines and habits: *If I'm late, my mother drives me to school.*
- Both clauses are about **general time** (always), not one future moment.

### Pronunciation

- Intonation rises a little at the end of the *if* clause and falls at the end of the sentence: *If you heat ice ↗, it melts ↘.*
- *-s* endings: *melts* /s/, *boils* /z/, *dies* /z/, *freezes* /ɪz/.

### Concept checking questions

*If you heat ice, it melts.*

1. Is this true only today? (No, always.)
2. Do we heat the ice first? (Yes.) Then what happens? (It melts.)

*If I'm tired, I go to bed early.*

1. Is this about one night or every time I'm tired? (Every time.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing verb be (If I tired). | Board If I am tired and drill. |
| Missing third-person -s (If water get cold, it freeze). | Highlight -s in a different colour; quick drill. |
| Using will (If you heat ice, it will melt). | Accept as understandable but teach the zero conditional for "always true" facts. |
| Comma confusion. | Show both orders on the board. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students with simple real-life "experiments". **Interaction:** T → Ss, whole class

1. Show a glass of water and an ice cube (or a picture). Ask: *"What happens if I put the ice in the sun?"* Students answer freely (*It melts / water*).
2. Show the plant. Ask: *"What happens if I don't give it water?"* (*It dies.*)
3. Say: *"Today we talk about things that always happen."*

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Part A: Context (8 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To present the target language in context. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the science facts. Match the beginnings and the endings."* (4 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"Do you write sentences or match?"* (Match.) *"Alone or in pairs?"* (Alone first.)
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class. Students read the full sentences aloud.

**Part B: Language clarification (12 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. **Meaning.** Board *If you heat ice, it melts.* Ask the CCQs. Draw two boxes with an arrow: *heat ice → melts*. Say: *"Always. Every time."*
2. **Form.** Underline both verbs. Ask: *"Past, present or future?"* (Present, both.) Write *If + present, present* in the centre of the board.
3. **Order.** Board *Ice melts if you heat it.* Ask: *"Same meaning?"* (Yes.) *"Where is the comma?"* (No comma.)
4. **-s and be.** Board *If water* ***gets*** *cold, it* ***freezes.*** and *If I* ***am*** *tired, I go to bed.* Highlight -s and *am*.
5. **Routines.** Give a personal example: *"If I'm hungry at school, I eat a banana."* Ask two students: *"What do you do if you're hungry?"*
6. **Pronunciation.** Model the intonation and drill chorally and individually: *If you heat ice ↗, it melts ↘. If I'm tired ↗, I go to bed ↘.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: write the verbs. Exercise B: correct the mistakes. Six minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Present or past?"* (Present.) *"Remember -s for he, she, it?"* (Yes.)
2. Pairs check. Nominate students to read their answers.
3. **Quick chain drill:** say the start of a sentence (*If you don't sleep, ...*); students finish it.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the zero conditional in speaking and writing about facts and routines. **Interaction:** Mingle, then pairs, then individual

1. **Find your partner (6 minutes).** Give each student one card from Handout 3 (a beginning or an ending). Students stand up, read their card and find the person with the matching half. Pairs read their full sentence to the class.
   - ICQs: *"Do you show your card or read it?"* (Read it.)
2. **My routines (8 minutes).** Give out Handout 4. Students complete the sentences about themselves, then ask a partner: *"What do you do if you're bored?"* They write the partner's answers in the "My partner" column.
3. **Writing (4 minutes).** Students write 3 sentences about their partner: *If Kasun is bored, he plays cricket.*
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Two or three students read their sentences about their partner.
2. Board four sentences from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write 6 sentences with *if*: 3 science facts and 3 sentences about your family's routines (*If my father is tired, he watches TV.*).

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the verbs for Handout 4 in a box (*eat, sleep, drink, call, play, read*).
- Allow them to write only the main clause in Handout 4.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *When* and *unless* (preview): *Plants die unless you water them.*
- They write a two-step fact: *If you heat water, it boils, and if it boils, it makes steam.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Students use the present simple in both clauses in their written sentences.
- Students match and read sentences correctly in the card game.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students remember the -s in the main clause?
- Was the difference between facts and routines clear?
