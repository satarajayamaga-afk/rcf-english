GRAMMAR TOPIC: PREPOSITIONS · B1 (Intermediate) · 60 minutes

# Prepositions of Movement and Transport

*Lesson theme: A Walking Tour of Kandy*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Prepositions |
| Grammar point | Prepositions of Movement and Transport |
| Level | B1 (Intermediate) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Intermediate students who know prepositions of place but confuse across / through, into / in, and transport phrases (by foot, get out the bus). |
| Main aim | To help students discover and use prepositions of movement (across, along, through, over, past, into, out of, up, down, towards, around) and transport phrases (by bus, on foot, get on / off, get into / out of). |
| Subsidiary aims | To follow a route on a map; to give and follow directions; to describe a journey. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can give clear directions on a map and describe their journey to school using at least six prepositions of movement. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (walking tour text and map), Handout 2 (discovery task with arrow diagrams), Handout 3 (practice), Handout 4 (information gap maps). |

## Language analysis

### Meaning

| **Preposition** | **Meaning** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| across | from one side to the other (on a surface) | Walk across the bridge / the road / the field. |
| through | from one end to the other, inside something | Go through the park / the tunnel / the forest. |
| along | following a line | Walk along the lake / the river / the road. |
| over | above and across | Climb over the wall. Fly over the city. |
| past | going by something | Go past the temple. |
| into / out of | entering / leaving | Walk into the museum. Come out of the shop. |
| up / down | to a higher / lower place | Go up the hill. Come down the steps. |
| towards | in the direction of | Walk towards the lake. |
| around | in a circle / on the other side | Walk around the lake. Turn around the corner. |

### Transport

| **by + transport (no article)** | **on foot** | **get on / off** | **get into / out of** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| by bus, by train, by car, by tuk-tuk | I go to school on foot. | bus, train, plane, bike | car, taxi, tuk-tuk |

### Pronunciation

- Weak *to* and *of*: *towards* /təˈwɔːdz/; *out of* /ˈaʊt əv/.

### Concept checking questions

*We walked through the park.* — Were we inside the park? (Yes.) *We walked across the road.* — Were we on the surface, from one side to the other? (Yes.) *I get off the bus.* — Big vehicle or small car? (Big, you can stand in it.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| across / through confusion. | Arrow diagrams: surface (across) vs inside (through). |
| by foot | Teach on foot as fixed. |
| get out the bus / get off the car | Stand-up vs sit-down rule. |
| by the bus | No article after by for transport. |

## Board plan

| **Left: arrows** | **Centre: rules** | **Right: transport** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| → across (surface) · → through (inside) · → along (line) · ↷ over · → past · ↘ into / ↗ out of · ↑ up / ↓ down · ↻ around · → towards | Surface: across · Inside: through · Line: along · Entering: into · Leaving: out of | by bus / train / car (no article) · on foot · get on / off (bus, train, plane) · get into / out of (car, taxi) |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage interest. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Mime movements: walking through a door, across the room, around a desk, past a student. Students guess the movement.
2. Ask: *"How do you come to school? What do you go past?"*

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present prepositions of movement and transport phrases in context and help students work out their meanings. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the walking tour. Draw the route on the map."*
   - ICQs: *"Where do you start?"* (The Clock Tower.) *"Do you write or draw?"* (Draw the route.)
2. Pairs compare their routes. Check as a class.

**Part B: Finding the meanings (7 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 2. Pairs match each preposition to its meaning (Part A) and answer the questions about transport (Part B).
2. Monitor and prompt: *"Were they inside the park, or on the surface of the road?"*

**Part C: Clarification (7 minutes)**

1. Elicit matches and draw the arrows on the board.
2. Ask the CCQs from the Language analysis. Focus on *across / through*.
3. Elicit the transport rules. Ask: *"Do we say by the bus?"* (No.) *"By foot?"* (No, on foot.)
4. Drill: *Go across the bridge. Walk along the lake. Go past the temple. I go to school on foot.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 3. Say: *"Exercise A: complete with the correct preposition. Exercise B: correct the mistakes. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs justify. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the language fluently in giving directions and describing journeys. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 4. Student A is a tourist; Student B is a local. Tourists ask for directions to three places; locals guide them using the map (10 minutes, swap after 5).
   - ICQs: *"Who has the map?"* (The local.) *"How many places?"* (Three.)
2. Students describe their own journey to school to a partner, using six prepositions and a transport phrase, then write it on Handout 4 (6 minutes).
3. Two students share with the class (2 minutes).

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board errors and good sentences; students correct the errors.
2. Mime three movements; students say the preposition.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write directions from your school to your home (120 words), using at least eight prepositions of movement.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Keep arrow diagrams on the board; give a direction phrase card.
- Weaker students give directions to one place only.

**Stretch**

- Add distances and landmarks: *about 200 metres, on your left, opposite the bank*.
- Describe an exciting journey using past tenses and movement prepositions.

## Assessment and success criteria

- Partners reach the correct places using the directions.
- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 3, Exercise A.

## Teacher's reflection

- Was the *across / through* distinction clear?
- Did the information gap produce accurate directions?

# Handout 1: A Walking Tour of Kandy

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Start at the Clock Tower.** Walk **along** Dalada Veediya **towards** the lake. Go **past** the Queen's Hotel on your right. Then walk **across** the road (carefully!) to Kandy Lake.

Walk **around** the lake until you reach the Temple of the Tooth. Go **into** the temple and spend some time inside. When you come **out of** the temple, walk **through** the Royal Palace Park.

Climb **up** the steps to the viewpoint and enjoy the view of the city. Then come **down** the hill and walk **over** the small bridge to the market. Finish your tour with a cup of tea!
