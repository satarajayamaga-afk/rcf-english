# Causative Have and Get (have something done) and the Get Passive

*Lesson theme: Services, Favours and Bad Luck*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | The Passive |
| Grammar point | Causative Have and Get (have something done) and the Get Passive |
| Level | B2 (Upper-intermediate) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Upper-intermediate students who use the passive in all tenses. When they talk about services they say I cut my hair yesterday (meaning a hairdresser did it) or I had cut my hair, and they don't know the get passive (get stolen, get fired). |
| Main aim | To develop students' use of the causative (have / get + object + past participle) for services, have something done for bad experiences, and the get passive in informal English. |
| Subsidiary aims | To develop fluency in describing errands and misfortunes; to practise sentence stress in causative sentences. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can talk about services they use and bad things that have happened, e.g. I'm having my phone screen repaired. My cousin had his bag stolen on the train. He got fined for parking. |

## Language analysis

### Form

| **Structure** | **Form** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Causative have | have + object + past participle | I had my hair cut yesterday. |
| Causative get (informal) | get + object + past participle | I need to get my car serviced. |
| All tenses | change have / get | I'm having my kitchen painted. I've just had my eyes tested. I'll get it fixed tomorrow. |
| Question / negative | do / did + have | Where did you have your photo taken? I didn't have it delivered. |
| Bad experience | have + object + past participle | She had her bag stolen. (Nobody wanted this!) |
| Get passive (informal) | get + past participle | He got fired. Our flight got cancelled. My phone got broken. |

### Meaning and use

- Causative: **someone else** does the action for us, usually a service we pay for. *I cut my hair* = I did it myself. *I had my hair cut* = a hairdresser did it.
- *have something done* can also describe something bad that happened to us (not arranged): *We had our car broken into.*
- *get* passive: informal; often for unexpected or negative events: *get hurt, get lost, get stolen, get caught, get fined, get promoted.*

### Pronunciation

- Stress the object and the participle, not *had*: *I had my HAIR CUT. She had her BAG STOLen.*
- Weak *had*: /həd/ or /əd/.

### Concept checking questions

*I had my hair cut yesterday.*

1. Did I cut my hair myself? (No.)
2. Who cut it? (A hairdresser.)
3. Did I pay? (Probably, yes.)

*Malik had his phone stolen.*

1. Did Malik want this to happen? (No.)
2. Did he arrange it? (No, it's a bad experience.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Wrong word order (I had cut my hair). | Board: have + OBJECT + pp; show that I had cut my hair is past perfect (I did it). |
| Using the active for services (I repaired my phone = a shop did it). | CCQ: "Did you do it yourself?" |
| Base verb instead of participle (I had my car wash). | Highlight the participle; quick drill. |
| Overusing get in formal writing. | Note: get is informal; use have or the be passive in formal writing. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To introduce the topic of services. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Write on the board: *hairdresser · optician · mechanic · photographer · tailor · phone repair shop*.
2. Ask: *"When did you last visit one of these? Why?"* Pairs discuss (2 minutes).
3. Take two answers and board one exactly as the student says it (e.g. *I cut my hair last week*).

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present the causative and the get passive in context and clarify their form and meaning. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read Kaveesha's blog. How many things did she have done? What went wrong? Three minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the four questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Underline every example of have or get + another verb. Who did each action: Kaveesha or someone else?"* Pairs compare.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. Board *I cut my hair* and *I had my hair cut.* Ask the CCQs. Elicit the form: *have + object + past participle.*
2. Show that *have* changes tense: *I'm having my phone repaired. I've had my eyes tested. I'll get it fixed.*
3. Show questions and negatives: *Where did you have it done? I didn't have it delivered.*
4. **Bad experiences.** Board *She had her bag stolen.* Ask the CCQs.
5. **get passive.** Elicit examples from the blog: *got broken, got soaked, got cancelled.* Note that they are informal.
6. Return to the student's sentence from Stage 1. Ask: *"Did you really cut it yourself?"* Correct it together.
7. **Pronunciation.** Drill with stress on the object and participle: *I had my HAIR CUT. She had her BAG STOLen.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the forms accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Read the reference box. Exercise A: write sentences with have something done. Exercise B: choose the correct form. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.
3. Fast finishers do Handout 3 (further practice); otherwise set it for homework.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the forms fluently in personalised speaking and storytelling. **Interaction:** Mingle, then groups

1. **Survey (8 minutes).** Give out Handout 4. Students mingle and ask the survey questions: *"Have you ever had your photo taken by a professional photographer?" "When did you last have your eyes tested?"* They ask one follow-up question each time.
   - ICQs: *"How many follow-up questions?"* (One for each question.)
2. **Bad luck stories (8 minutes).** In groups, students take a story card and tell a short (true or invented) story of bad luck, using at least one *have something done* and one *get* passive: *"Last year, I had my wallet stolen on the bus, and then I got fined because I didn't have my ticket!"* The group chooses the unluckiest story.
3. The group tells the unluckiest story to the class (2 minutes).
4. Monitor and note language use.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board sentences from Stage 4 (correct and incorrect). Students correct the errors.
2. Ask: *"What's the difference between I painted my house and I had my house painted?"*
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Complete Handout 3. Then write an email to a friend (150 words) about a busy week of errands that went wrong. Use *have / get something done* at least four times and the *get* passive twice.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the services vocabulary with pictures.
- Provide frames: *I had my ___ ___ed. I need to get my ___ ___ed.*

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use the causative with a person: *I had the mechanic check the brakes. I got my brother to fix it.*
- They use *need* + *-ing* / *need to be* + pp: *My car needs servicing.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 5 out of 6 or better on Handout 2, Exercise B.
- In the survey, students form causative questions with correct word order.
- Each story includes at least one causative and one *get* passive used accurately.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students stop confusing *I had my hair cut* and *I had cut my hair*?
- Was the *get* passive used naturally in the stories?

# Handout 1: Kaveesha's Busy Saturday

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**My Saturday of Errands (and Disasters!)**

Saturday was supposed to be easy. First, I had my hair cut at the salon on Galle Road. It looks great! Then I went to the optician to have my eyes tested, because I've been getting headaches. Good news: I don't need glasses.

Next, I took my laptop to the repair shop. The screen got broken last week when my little brother sat on it. They're going to get it fixed by Wednesday, but it'll cost me Rs. 15,000!

In the afternoon, I wanted to have some photos printed for my grandmother's birthday. But when I got to the bus stop, I realised I'd had my purse stolen. All my money and my bank card! I got soaked in the rain while I was walking to the police station, and then, when I got home, I found out that my evening flight to Jaffna had got cancelled.

Next Saturday, I'm staying in bed.

1. What did Kaveesha have done at the salon? ______________________________
2. Why did she have her eyes tested? ______________________________
3. What happened to her purse? ______________________________
4. What happened to her flight? ______________________________

# Handout 2: Language Focus and Practice

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Language reference**

| **Use** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- |
| A service (someone does it for you) | I had my hair cut. I'm going to get my car washed. |
| A bad experience | She had her purse stolen. |
| get passive (informal) | The flight got cancelled. He got fined. |
| Remember the word order | have / get + OBJECT + past participle |

**A. Write sentences with have something done in the tense given.**

1. A mechanic is repairing my car. (present continuous) → I ______________________________
2. A photographer took our wedding photos. (past simple) → We ______________________________
3. Someone is going to paint our house. (going to) → We ______________________________
4. Someone broke into my uncle's house last night. (past simple) → My uncle ______________________________

**B. Choose the correct form.**

1. I can't come. I **am cutting my hair / am having my hair cut** at 4 o'clock.
2. Where did you **have your suit made / have made your suit**?
3. He **got fined / got fine** for driving too fast.
4. I **had repaired my phone / had my phone repaired** last week. It works perfectly now.
5. You should **get checked your teeth / get your teeth checked** every six months.
6. Last night, my neighbour **stole his car / had his car stolen** from outside his house.
