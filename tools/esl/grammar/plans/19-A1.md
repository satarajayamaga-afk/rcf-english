GRAMMAR TOPIC: USED TO, WOULD AND BE / GET USED TO · A1 (Beginner, upper end) · 60 minutes

# Used To for Past Habits and States (I used to play ..., I didn't use to ...)

*Lesson theme: When I Was a Child*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Used To, Would and Be / Get Used To |
| Grammar point | Used To for Past Habits and States (I used to play ..., I didn't use to ...) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner, upper end) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | High-A1 learners who know the present simple and some past simple forms (was, had, played). To talk about childhood they use the present (When I was a child I play with my brother) or the past simple only, and they have not met used to. When they try it, they add -s or -ed to the main verb (I used to played). |
| Main aim | To present and practise used to + base verb for things that were true or happened regularly in the past but not now, in positive and negative sentences (didn't use to). |
| Subsidiary aims | To contrast past and present (I used to ..., but now I ...); to practise the pronunciation /ˈjuːstə/; to develop speaking with childhood photos. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can talk about their childhood and compare it with now, e.g. I used to live in Matara. I used to play with my cousins every day. I didn't use to like vegetables, but now I love them. |
| Materials | Childhood photos (the teacher's and students', if possible), Handout 1 (Grandpa's life), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (then and now chart). |

## Language analysis

### Form

|  | **Form** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Positive | subject + used to + base verb | I used to play cricket. She used to have long hair. |
| Negative | subject + didn't use to + base verb | I didn't use to like fish. |

- The main verb is the **base form**: *used to play* (not *used to played*).
- *used to* is the same for all persons: *I / you / he / she / we / they used to*.
- Negative: *didn't use to* (no *d*), because *didn't* already shows the past.

### Meaning

- Something was true or happened again and again in the past, **but not now**.
- *I used to live in Matara* = I lived there before; I don't live there now.

### Pronunciation

- *used to* /ˈjuːstə/ (one sound group: "yoos-tuh"). The *d* is silent.

### Concept checking questions

*I used to live in Matara.*

1. Did I live in Matara before? (Yes.) Do I live there now? (No.)

*She used to have long hair.*

1. Is her hair long now? (No.) Was it long before? (Yes, for a long time.)

*I didn't use to like fish.*

1. Did I like fish before? (No.) Do I like it now? (Yes, probably.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Past form after used to (used to played). | Board: used to + base verb; cross out played. |
| Pronunciation /juːzd tuː/. | Drill /ˈjuːstə/ as one chunk. |
| didn't used to | Board: didn't + use (no d). |
| Using it for one action (I used to go to Kandy last week). | Timeline: many times / a long time, not one time. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students with the topic. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Show a photo of yourself as a child (or a drawing). Ask: *"Who is this? How old was I?"*
2. Students guess. Say: *"Yes, it's me! I was 7."*

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present *used to* in context and clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class, individual, pairs

**Part A: Teacher's story (6 minutes)**

1. Talk about the photo: *"When I was a child, I used to live in a village. I used to walk to school. I didn't use to like rice! Now I love it."* Draw a timeline: then ← → now.
2. Ask the CCQs.

**Part B: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read about Grandpa Somapala. How was his life different? Three minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Who is the text about?"* (Grandpa Somapala.)
2. Students answer the true / false questions. Pairs compare. Check as a class.

**Part C: Clarification (8 minutes)**

1. Board two sentences from the text. Highlight *used to* + base verb.
2. Board the negative *didn't use to*. Cross out *didn't used to* and *used to played*.
3. Show that it's the same for all persons.
4. **Pronunciation.** Drill /ˈjuːstə/: *I used to PLAY. He used to WALK. We didn't use to have a TV.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: complete with used to or didn't use to. Exercise B: correct the mistakes. Six minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"used to + play or played?"* (Play.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use *used to* to talk about real childhood. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. **Then and now (5 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. Students complete the chart about themselves: where they lived, food, games, school, hair, bedtime.
2. **Tell your partner (7 minutes).** Students tell a partner: *"I used to live in Galle, but now I live in Colombo. I used to play hide and seek."* The partner asks: *"Really? Did you like it?"*
3. **Guess who (6 minutes).** Students write one sentence about their childhood on a slip of paper. The teacher reads them out; the class guesses who wrote each: *"I used to have a pet monkey!"*
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick round: each student says one thing they used to do.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Ask a grandparent or older person about their childhood. Write 6 sentences with *used to* and *didn't use to*: *My grandmother used to cook on a wood fire.*

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the then-and-now pictures with verbs.
- Give the frame: *I used to ..., but now I ...*

**Stretch**

- Stronger students ask questions: *Did you use to ...?*
- They use *never used to*: *I never used to eat breakfast.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2.
- Students use *used to* + base verb, and *didn't use to* in negatives.
- Students pronounce *used to* as /ˈjuːstə/.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did the timeline make the "not now" meaning clear?
- Did students enjoy sharing childhood memories?

# Handout 1: Grandpa Somapala

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

My grandpa Somapala is 80. When he was a child, he lived in a small village near Anuradhapura.

He used to wake up at 5 o'clock every day. He used to help his father in the paddy fields before school. He didn't use to wear shoes; he used to walk to school with no shoes! His family didn't use to have electricity, so they used to read with an oil lamp.

Now Grandpa lives in Colombo with us. He has a smartphone and he watches cricket on TV every evening. But he still wakes up at 5!

**True (T) or False (F)?**

1. Grandpa used to live in Colombo. ______
2. He used to help his father in the fields. ______
3. He used to wear shoes to school. ______
4. His family didn't use to have electricity. ______
5. Grandpa doesn't wake up early now. ______
