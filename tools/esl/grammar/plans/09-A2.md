GRAMMAR TOPIC: THE PASSIVE · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# Past Simple Passive (was built, were invented)

*Lesson theme: Famous Places and Great Inventions*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | The Passive |
| Grammar point | Past Simple Passive (was built, were invented) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who know the present simple passive and the past simple. They want to talk about history but say Sigiriya built in the 5th century or The telephone was invent by Bell. |
| Main aim | To present and practise the past simple passive (was / were + past participle), with and without by + agent, to talk about famous places and inventions. |
| Subsidiary aims | To review irregular past participles; to read short fact files for specific information; to develop speaking through a history quiz. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can write and ask quiz questions such as When was the telephone invented? Who was Sigiriya built by? and answer them in full sentences. |
| Materials | Pictures of Sigiriya, a telephone, a light bulb and the Taj Mahal (or descriptions), Handout 1 (fact files), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (quiz cards A and B), Handout 4 (write your own quiz). |

## Language analysis

### Form

|  | **Singular** | **Plural** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Positive | Sigiriya was built in the 5th century. | The first cars were made in Germany. |
| Negative | It wasn't built by the British. | Phones weren't invented in 1700. |
| Question | When was the telephone invented? | Where were the first cars made? |

- **was / were + past participle**.
- **by** + agent when we want to say who did it: *The telephone was invented* ***by*** *Alexander Graham Bell.*
- Question about the agent: *Who was it invented by?*

### Meaning and use

- We use the passive when the place or object is the topic, and the person is less important, unknown or obvious: *The temple was built in 1592.*
- We add *by* + agent only when the person is important or interesting.

### Pronunciation

- Weak *was* /wəz/ and *were* /wə/: *It was built* /ɪt wəz bɪlt/.
- *-ed* endings: *invented* /ɪd/, *discovered* /d/, *painted* /ɪd/.

### Concept checking questions

*Sigiriya was built in the 5th century.*

1. Is this about the past or now? (The past.)
2. Did Sigiriya build itself? (No, people built it.)
3. What is the topic of the sentence: Sigiriya or the builders? (Sigiriya.)

*The telephone was invented by Bell.*

1. Who invented it? (Bell.)
2. Why do we say *by Bell*? (Because the person is important.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing was / were (Sigiriya built in ...). | Board the two-part formula; drill. |
| Base verb or past simple instead of participle (was invent, was wrote). | Participle list; quick-fire drill. |
| was with plurals. | Singular / plural contrast in Exercise A. |
| Overusing by + agent (by people, by someone). | Rule: only when the agent is important. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students and activate knowledge. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. Show the four pictures. Ask: *"What do you know about these? How old are they?"* Pairs discuss (2 minutes).
2. Take a few ideas. Don't correct yet.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present the past simple passive in context and clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the fact files. Find one date and one name in each. Three minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"How many fact files?"* (Four.)
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the four questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Underline was or were + another verb."* Pairs compare. Elicit examples and board them.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. **Meaning.** Board *King Kashyapa built Sigiriya* and *Sigiriya was built in the 5th century.* Ask the CCQs.
2. **Form.** Elicit *was / were + past participle*. Highlight *were* with plurals.
3. **by + agent.** Board *The telephone was invented by Bell.* Ask: *"Why do we say by Bell here?"* Contrast with *The temple was built in 1592* (agent not needed).
4. **Questions.** Board *When was the telephone invented? Who was it invented by?*
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill weak forms: *It was built in 1592. When was it invented? They were made in Germany.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: was or were + past participle. Exercise B: rewrite the sentences in the passive. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the past simple passive in a history quiz. **Interaction:** Pairs, then teams

1. **Information gap (8 minutes).** Give Student A and Student B the quiz cards (Handout 3). Each card has answers the partner needs. Students ask and answer: *"When was the first photograph taken?" "It was taken in 1826."*
   - ICQs: *"Can you show your card?"* (No.) *"Do you answer in a full sentence?"* (Yes.)
2. **Write a quiz (10 minutes).** Give out Handout 4. Teams write five quiz questions about places and inventions in their own country or the world, then ask another team.
3. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences heard in Stage 4 (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Ask: *"When do we use by?"*
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write a fact file (80 words) about a famous place or invention. Use the past simple passive at least four times.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the participle list during the quiz.
- Provide question frames: *When was ___ built / invented? Who was ___ painted by?*

**Stretch**

- Stronger students add extra facts with the present simple passive: *It is visited by thousands of tourists every year.*
- They write negative sentences that correct false facts: *The light bulb wasn't invented by Newton.*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Students form past passive questions correctly in the quiz.
- Students use *by* + agent only when the agent is important.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students use the past participle, not the past simple form?
- Did the quiz motivate accurate questions?

# Handout 1: Fact Files

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Sigiriya** The rock fortress of Sigiriya was built in the 5th century by King Kashyapa. Beautiful paintings were made on the rock walls. Today, it is visited by thousands of tourists every year.

**The Telephone** The telephone was invented in 1876 by Alexander Graham Bell. The first words on the phone were spoken to his assistant: "Come here, I want to see you."

**The Light Bulb** The first electric lights were made in the early 1800s, but the first useful light bulb was designed by Thomas Edison in 1879.

**The Taj Mahal** The Taj Mahal was built in India between 1632 and 1653. It was made of white marble, and it was designed as a tomb for the emperor's wife.

**Answer the questions.**

1. Who was Sigiriya built by? ______________________________
2. When was the telephone invented? ______________________________
3. Who was the first useful light bulb designed by? ______________________________
4. What was the Taj Mahal made of? ______________________________
