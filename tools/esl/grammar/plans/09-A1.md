GRAMMAR TOPIC: THE PASSIVE · A1 (Beginner) · 60 minutes

# Present Simple Passive (is made, are grown)

*Lesson theme: Made in Sri Lanka*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | The Passive |
| Grammar point | Present Simple Passive (is made, are grown) |
| Level | A1 (Beginner) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | High beginners who know the present simple, am / is / are and common regular and irregular verbs. They want to say where things come from but say Tea is grow in Sri Lanka or Tea grows by farmers. |
| Main aim | To present and practise the present simple passive (is / are + past participle) to talk about products and where they are made, grown or sold. |
| Subsidiary aims | To learn eight common past participles (made, grown, sold, eaten, spoken, used, produced, caught); to develop speaking through a quiz. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can say and write 6 sentences about products, e.g. Tea is grown in Nuwara Eliya. Cars are made in Japan. |
| Materials | Real objects or pictures (a packet of tea, a coconut, a cricket bat, a mobile phone, a banana), Handout 1 (product labels), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (Where is it from? quiz). |

## Language analysis

### Form

|  | **Singular** | **Plural** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Positive | Tea is grown in Sri Lanka. | Phones are made in China. |
| Negative | Rice isn't grown in the desert. | Cars aren't made here. |
| Question | Where is coffee grown? | Where are these shoes made? |

- **is / are + past participle**.
- Past participles: *make → made, grow → grown, sell → sold, eat → eaten, speak → spoken, use → used, produce → produced, catch → caught*.

### Meaning and use

- We use the passive when the **thing** is more important than the person who does the action, or when we don't know or care who does it: *Tea is grown in the hills.* (Who grows it? Farmers. Not important.)

### Pronunciation

- Weak *is* and *are*: *Tea's grown* /tiːz ɡrəʊn/; *are made* /ə meɪd/.
- *-ed* participles: *used* /juːzd/, *produced* /prəˈdjuːst/.

### Concept checking questions

*Tea is grown in Sri Lanka.*

1. Does tea grow itself? (No, people grow it.)
2. Who grows it? (Farmers.) Is that important here? (No.)
3. What is important? (The tea and the place.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Missing past participle (Tea is grow). | Board is + grown; drill base verb → participle. |
| Missing is / are (Tea grown in Sri Lanka). | Two-part formula on the board with colours. |
| is with plurals (Phones is made). | Singular / plural contrast in practice. |
| Using the active wrongly (Tea grows by farmers). | Show who does the action vs what happens to the thing. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To create interest and introduce the topic. **Interaction:** T → Ss, whole class

1. Show the objects one by one. Ask: *"What's this? Where is it from?"* Students guess freely.
2. Hold up the tea. Say: *"Sri Lankan tea is famous!"* Ask: *"Where in Sri Lanka?"* (*Nuwara Eliya, Kandy ...*)

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Part A: Context (8 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To present the language in context. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the labels. Match each label to a product."* (3 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"Do you match or write?"* (Match.)
2. Pairs compare. Check as a class.
3. Students answer the three questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Language clarification (12 minutes)**

**Stage aim:** To clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. **Meaning.** Board *Farmers grow tea.* and *Tea is grown in Sri Lanka.* Ask the CCQs. Point: *"In the second sentence, the tea is more important."*
2. **Form.** Underline *is grown*. Elicit *is / are + past participle*. Board *Phones are made in China* to show *are* with plurals.
3. **Participles.** Elicit participles from the labels and complete the list on the left of the board. Drill *grow – grown, make – made*.
4. **Negatives and questions.** Board *Rice isn't grown in the desert* and *Where is coffee grown?*
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill weak forms: *Tea's grown in Sri Lanka. Phones are made in China. Where's coffee grown?*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: is or are + past participle. Exercise B: make questions. Six minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Do you write the base verb or the past participle?"* (Past participle.)
2. Pairs check. Nominate students to read answers.
3. **Quick drill:** say a product and a verb (*rice / grow*); students say the sentence (*Rice is grown in Sri Lanka.*).

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the present simple passive in speaking and writing. **Interaction:** Teams, then individual

1. **Quiz (10 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. In teams, students choose the correct country for each product and write a full sentence: *Kiwis are grown in New Zealand.* Then teams ask each other the questions aloud: *"Where are kiwis grown?"*
   - ICQs: *"Do you write the country or a full sentence?"* (A full sentence.)
2. Check answers. One point for the correct fact, one for a correct sentence.
3. **Writing (6 minutes).** Students write 4 sentences about products from their town or country at the bottom of Handout 3: *Coconuts are grown in Kurunegala. Batik is made in Kandy.*
4. Two students read their sentences (2 minutes).

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from the quiz and writing (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick round: each student says one thing that is made or grown in their country.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Look at 6 things in your kitchen. Write where each one is made or grown: *My rice is grown in Polonnaruwa. My fridge is made in Korea.*

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the participle list on a card.
- Allow them to complete sentence frames: *___ is grown in ___.*

**Stretch**

- Stronger students add *by* + agent when it is important: *Batik is made by hand.*
- They ask and answer *What language is spoken in ...?*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 8 out of 10 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- Students use *is / are* + past participle in their own sentences.
- Students choose *is* or *are* correctly for singular and plural.

## Teacher's reflection

- Which participles were hardest?
- Did students understand why we use the passive?
