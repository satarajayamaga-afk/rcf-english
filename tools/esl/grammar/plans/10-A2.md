GRAMMAR TOPIC: REPORTED SPEECH · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# Reported Statements in the Past (said that, told me that)

*Lesson theme: Celebrity Interviews*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Reported Speech |
| Grammar point | Reported Statements in the Past (said that, told me that) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who can report messages in the present (She says she's tired). When they report what someone said earlier, they don't change the tense (He said he is tired) and they confuse said and told (He said me). |
| Main aim | To present and practise reported statements with said (that) and told + person (that), with the main tense changes (present → past, will → would, can → could) and pronoun changes. |
| Subsidiary aims | To read an interview for specific information; to develop speaking through a "class interview" activity. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can report what a classmate said in an interview, e.g. Nimal said he was a good swimmer. He told me he would visit Japan next year. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (magazine interview with a young cricketer), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (interview questions and report sheet). |

## Language analysis

### Form: the tense moves back

| **Direct speech** | **Reported speech** |
| :-- | :-- |
| "I am tired." | She said (that) she was tired. |
| "I like cricket." | He said he liked cricket. |
| "I'm playing tomorrow." | He said he was playing the next day. |
| "I will win." | She said she would win. |
| "I can swim." | He said he could swim. |

- **said** + (that) + clause. **told** + person + (that) + clause.
- Pronouns and possessives change: *I → he / she, my → his / her, we → they*.
- Time words often change: *today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, now → then*.

### Meaning and use

- We use *said / told* + past forms to report what someone said at an earlier time.

### Pronunciation

- *said* /sed/, *told* /təʊld/. *that* is weak /ðət/ or left out.

### Concept checking questions

*Interview: "I'm very nervous." → She said she was very nervous.*

1. When did she speak: now or before? (Before, in the interview.)
2. Is *was* about the past? (It shows the words were said before; she was nervous at the time of the interview.)

*He told me he could swim.*

1. Who did he speak to? (Me.)
2. Can I say *He said me*? (No.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| No tense change (He said he is tired). | Board a "step back" arrow: am → was, like → liked. |
| said me | Board: say + what; tell + who + what. |
| Forgetting pronoun changes. | Colour-code I → she. |
| Students find time words confusing. | Teach only tomorrow → the next day and today → that day. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To create interest in the topic. **Interaction:** Whole class, pairs

1. Ask: *"Which famous sports stars do you like? If you met one, what would you ask?"* Pairs list three questions (2 minutes).
2. Take a few questions and board two.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present reported statements in context and clarify meaning, form and pronunciation. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read the interview with Shenali, a young cricketer. Then read the magazine report. Answer the questions."* (4 minutes.)
   - ICQs: *"How many texts?"* (Two: the interview and the report.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Find the same information in the interview and in the report. What changes?"* Pairs compare three sentences.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. **Meaning.** Board *"I'm very nervous." → She said she was very nervous.* Ask the CCQs.
2. **Tense changes.** Elicit the changes from the handout and build the list on the left of the board. Draw a "step back" arrow.
3. **Pronouns and time.** Highlight *I → she, my → her, tomorrow → the next day.*
4. **said and told.** Board *She told the reporter she could bowl fast.* Ask: *"After told, what comes?"* (A person.) Board *She said me* ✗.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill: *She said she was nervous. He told me he could swim.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the form accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: report the sentences. Exercise B: said or told? Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To report what classmates said in a personalised interview. **Interaction:** Pairs, then new pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 3. **Interview (7 minutes).** Students interview a partner with the questions and write the exact answers in the "Answer" column.
   - ICQs: *"Do you write the exact words or a report?"* (The exact words.)
2. **Report (7 minutes).** Students change partners and report what their first partner said: *"Nimal said he was a good swimmer. He told me he would visit Japan next year."*
3. **Writing (4 minutes).** Students write four sentences in the report section of Handout 3.
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Two students report the most surprising thing their partner said.
2. Board four sentences from Stage 4 (two with errors). Students correct them.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Ask a family member five questions about their childhood. Write what they said, using *said* and *told me*.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the "step back" list on a card.
- Let them report only present simple and *be* sentences.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students report past simple sentences with the past perfect (preview): *She said she had played for three years.*
- They use more time changes: *yesterday → the day before, next week → the following week*.

## Assessment and success criteria

- 6 out of 8 or better on Handout 2, Exercise A.
- In the report, students change the tense and pronoun correctly in most sentences.
- Students use *told* + person and *said* without a person.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did students remember to change the tense?
- Was the interview personal and interesting?

# Handout 1: Interview with a Young Cricketer

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**The interview**

**Reporter:** How old are you, Shenali? **Shenali:** I'm 16. **Reporter:** How do you feel about your first big match? **Shenali:** I'm very nervous, but I'm excited too. **Reporter:** What can you do well? **Shenali:** I can bowl very fast. My coach is my father. **Reporter:** What are your plans? **Shenali:** I'm playing in Galle tomorrow. One day I will play for Sri Lanka!

**The magazine report**

Shenali Perera said she was 16. She said she was very nervous about her first big match, but she was excited too. She told our reporter she could bowl very fast, and she said her coach was her father. She said she was playing in Galle the next day, and she told us she would play for Sri Lanka one day.

**Answer the questions.**

1. How old did Shenali say she was? ______________________________
2. How did she feel? ______________________________
3. Who did she say her coach was? ______________________________
4. Where did she say she was playing the next day? ______________________________

| :-- | :-- |
| He said he is tired. | He said he was tired. |
| She said me she was busy. | She told me she was busy. / She said she was busy. |

| She said she will come. | She said she would come. |
