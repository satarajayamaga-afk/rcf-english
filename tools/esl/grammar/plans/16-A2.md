GRAMMAR TOPIC: PHRASAL VERBS · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# Separable Phrasal Verbs with Object Pronouns (put it on, turn it off, pick them up)

*Lesson theme: House Rules and Tidying Up*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Phrasal Verbs |
| Grammar point | Separable Phrasal Verbs with Object Pronouns (put it on, turn it off, pick them up) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who know common phrasal verbs (get up, turn on, put on). When they replace the noun with a pronoun, they put it at the end (Turn off it! Pick up them!), and they don't know that a noun can go in two positions. |
| Main aim | To clarify and practise word order with separable phrasal verbs: noun before or after the particle (turn off the TV / turn the TV off), pronoun only between verb and particle (turn it off). |
| Subsidiary aims | To learn household phrasal verbs (pick up, put away, throw away, turn down, turn up, clean up, hang up, wash up); to practise imperatives for rules; to develop speaking in a role-play. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can give and respond to household instructions, e.g. "Please put your clothes away." "OK, I'll put them away now." "Turn the music down!" "Sorry, I'll turn it down." |
| Materials | Handout 1 (a note from Mum and a dialogue), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (role cards), a few real objects (a bag, a pen, a jacket). |

## Language analysis

### Form

|  | **Noun object** | **Pronoun object (it / them / him / her)** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ✓ | Turn off the TV. | Turn it off. |
| ✓ | Turn the TV off. | — |
| ✗ | — | Turn off it. |

- With a **noun**, the particle can go before or after the noun.
- With a **pronoun**, the pronoun must go **between** the verb and the particle: *pick them up, put it away, throw them away*.

### Household phrasal verbs

| **Phrasal verb** | **Meaning** |
| :-- | :-- |
| pick up | take something from the floor or a table |
| put away | put something in its usual place |
| throw away | put in the rubbish bin |
| turn up / turn down | make louder / quieter (TV, music) |
| clean up | make a place clean and tidy |
| hang up | put clothes on a hook or hanger |
| wash up | wash the plates and cups |
| take out | carry outside (the rubbish) |

- *Wash up* has no object in this lesson: *I'll wash up.*

### Pronunciation

- Stress the particle, not the pronoun: *Turn it OFF. Pick them UP.*
- Weak pronouns: *them* /ðəm/: *pick them up* /pɪk ðəm ˈʌp/.

### Concept checking questions

*Turn it off.*

1. What is *it*? (The TV / the music: something we know.)
2. Can I say *Turn off it*? (No.)

*Put your clothes away.*

1. Where do the clothes go? (In the cupboard, their usual place.)
2. Can I say *Put away your clothes*? (Yes.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Pronoun at the end (Turn off it). | Board a "sandwich": verb + pronoun + particle. |
| Thinking the noun can't separate the verb. | Show both positions are fine with a noun. |
| them for one thing / it for plurals. | Use real objects: one pen → it; two pens → them. |
| Confusing put away and throw away. | Mime: cupboard vs bin. |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students with the topic. **Interaction:** Pairs, whole class

1. Ask: *"Who does the housework in your home? What jobs do you do?"*
2. Pairs tell each other (2 minutes). Take ideas.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present separable phrasal verbs in context and clarify word order with pronouns. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Vocabulary (5 minutes)**

1. Drop a pen on the floor. Say: *"Oh! I need to pick up the pen."* Mime the other verbs with objects. Students repeat.

**Part B: Context (7 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read Mum's note and the dialogue. What jobs does Dilan do? Three minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the questions. Check as a class.
3. Say: *"Look at the dialogue. Find the words it and them. Where are they?"*

**Part C: Clarification (8 minutes)**

1. Board *Turn off the TV.* and *Turn the TV off.* Ask: *"Same meaning?"* (Yes.)
2. Replace *the TV* with *it*. Board *Turn it off* ✓ and *Turn off it* ✗. Draw the "sandwich". Ask the CCQs.
3. Use real objects: pick up a pen → *Pick it up*; pick up two pens → *Pick them up*.
4. **Pronunciation.** Drill *Turn it OFF. Pick them UP. Put it aWAY.*

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise word order accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: replace the words with it or them. Exercise B: tick or correct. Seven minutes."*
   - ICQs: *"Pick up the books: it or them?"* (Them.)
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To use the phrasal verbs fluently in a role-play. **Interaction:** Pairs, then whole class

1. **House rules (5 minutes).** Pairs write five house rules for a family or a student hostel: *Put your dishes away. Turn the music down after 10 pm.*
2. **Role-play (9 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. Student A is a parent (or hostel warden); Student B is a teenager. A gives instructions; B answers with a pronoun: *"Hang your towel up!" "OK, I'll hang it up."* B can give excuses: *"I can't take it out now, I'm doing my homework!"* Swap roles with the second card.
   - ICQs: *"When you answer, do you say the noun again?"* (No, use it or them.)
3. Two pairs perform for the class (4 minutes).
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick fire: say *"Pick up the books!"*; students answer *"OK, I'll pick them up."*
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write a short dialogue (8–10 lines) between a parent and a child about tidying the house. Use at least five phrasal verbs with *it* or *them*.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the "sandwich" diagram on a card.
- In the role-play, let them use only *it* first, then *them*.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *him / her / me*: *Can you pick me up from school? I'll wake her up.*
- They add *for me*: *Could you take the rubbish out for me?*

## Assessment and success criteria

- 10 out of 12 or better on Handout 2.
- In the role-play, students put the pronoun between the verb and the particle.
- Students choose *it* or *them* correctly.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did the "sandwich" image help students remember the word order?
- Was the role-play fun and realistic?

# Handout 1: A Note from Mum

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**Dilan,**

I'm at Aunty's house until 6. Please do these jobs:

- Put your school books away.
- Pick up your clothes from the floor and hang them up.
- Take the rubbish out.
- Don't turn the TV up too loud!

Thank you! **Mum**

**Later ...**

**Mum:** Dilan, I'm home! Did you do the jobs? **Dilan:** Yes! I put my books away, and I picked my clothes up and hung them up. **Mum:** Good. And the rubbish? **Dilan:** Oh no! I'll take it out now. **Mum:** And why is the TV so loud? Turn it down, please! **Dilan:** Sorry, Mum. I'll turn it down. **Mum:** And who left these cups here? Please wash them, then put them away.

**Answer the questions.**

1. Where is Mum until 6? ______________________________
2. Which job didn't Dilan do? ______________________________
3. What's the problem with the TV? ______________________________

# Handout 3: Role Cards

**Card 1**

**Student A (parent):** Give these instructions. Be strict!

- the music is too loud
- the towel is on the bed
- the plates are on the table
- the rubbish is in the kitchen

**Student B (teenager):** Answer with *it* or *them*. You can say yes, or give an excuse.

**Card 2**

**Student B (hostel warden):** Give these instructions.

- the TV is on and nobody is watching
- shoes are in the corridor
- the old bottles are under the bed
- the jackets are on the floor

**Student A (student):** Answer with *it* or *them*.

**Useful language:** *Please ... · Can you ...? · OK, I'll ... it / them ... now. · Sorry, I can't ... it / them ... now because ...*
