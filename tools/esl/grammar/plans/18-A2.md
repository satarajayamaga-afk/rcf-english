GRAMMAR TOPIC: LINKING WORDS · A2 (Elementary) · 60 minutes

# So vs Because, and Sequencing Words (first, then, after that, finally)

*Lesson theme: A Terrible Day*

## Lesson overview

|  |  |
| :-- | :-- |
| Grammar topic | Linking Words |
| Grammar point | So vs Because, and Sequencing Words (first, then, after that, finally) |
| Level | A2 (Elementary) |
| Duration | 60 minutes |
| Class profile | Elementary students who use and, but and because accurately and the past simple for stories. They confuse cause and result (I was hungry because I ate a sandwich), don't use so, and tell stories as lists with and then and then. |
| Main aim | To clarify and practise because (reason) vs so (result), and sequencing words (first, then, next, after that, finally) to organise a short past narrative. |
| Subsidiary aims | To practise the past simple in stories; to practise punctuation with so and sequencers; to develop speaking and writing through a picture story. |
| Learning outcome | By the end of the lesson, students can tell and write a short story in order with reasons and results, e.g. First, my alarm didn't ring, so I woke up late. Then I missed the bus because I couldn't find my shoes. Finally, I got to school at 9. |
| Materials | Handout 1 (Kavindu's terrible day), Handout 2 (practice), Handout 3 (picture story), linking word cards. |

## Language analysis

### because vs so

|  | **Form** | **Example** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| because + reason | result + because + reason | I was late because I missed the bus. |
| so + result | reason + , so + result | I missed the bus, so I was late. |

- Same situation, opposite order: the reason comes after *because* and before *so*.
- A comma usually goes before *so*.

### Sequencing words

| **Order** | **Words** | **Punctuation** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| beginning | First, / First of all, | comma after |
| middle | Then / Next, / After that, | comma after Next and After that (optional after Then) |
| end | Finally, / In the end, | comma after |

- Don't repeat *and then* in every sentence; vary the words.

### Pronunciation

- Pause after sequencers: *First, | my alarm didn't ring.*
- *so* is stressed when it introduces an important result: *..., SO I was late.*

### Concept checking questions

*I missed the bus, so I was late.*

1. What happened first: I missed the bus or I was late? (I missed the bus.) What was the result? (I was late.)

*I was late because I missed the bus.*

1. Why was I late? (Because I missed the bus.) Is this the same situation? (Yes.)

## Anticipated problems and solutions

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Cause and result reversed. | Arrow diagram: reason → so → result; result ← because ← reason. |
| No comma before so. | Model punctuation; underline commas. |
| and then repeated. | Sequencing ladder on the board; challenge to use four different words. |
| Finally used for "at last after waiting" only. | Teach Finally as the last step in a story. |

## Board plan

| **Left: because / so** | **Centre: examples** | **Right: sequencing** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| reason → , so → result · result → because → reason | I missed the bus, so I was late. · I was late because I missed the bus. · My alarm didn't ring, so I woke up late. · not: I was hungry because I ate a sandwich ✗ (= I ate a sandwich because I was hungry) | First, ... · Then ... · Next, ... · After that, ... · Finally, ... · pause after each · not: and then, and then, and then ✗ |

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Warm-up (5 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To engage students with the topic. **Interaction:** Pairs, whole class

1. Ask: *"Have you ever had a really bad day? What happened?"*
2. Pairs share (2 minutes). Take one or two stories.

### Stage 2: Presentation (20 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To present *so*, *because* and sequencers in a story and clarify meaning and punctuation. **Interaction:** Individual, pairs, whole class

**Part A: Reading (6 minutes)**

1. Give out Handout 1. Say: *"Read about Kavindu's terrible day. Put the pictures in order. Three minutes."*
2. Pairs compare. Students answer the questions. Check as a class.

**Part B: Noticing (4 minutes)**

1. Say: *"Find so and because in the story. Underline the reason in red and the result in blue."* Pairs compare.

**Part C: Clarification (10 minutes)**

1. Board *I missed the bus, so I was late.* and *I was late because I missed the bus.* Draw the arrow diagram. Ask the CCQs.
2. Highlight the comma before *so*.
3. Build the sequencing ladder from the story. Show how they help the reader.
4. Board *I was hungry because I ate a sandwich* ✗. Ask: *"Is this logical?"* Correct it.
5. **Pronunciation.** Drill with a pause after sequencers.

### Stage 3: Controlled practice (10 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To practise the forms accurately. **Interaction:** Individual, then pairs, then whole class

1. Give out Handout 2. Say: *"Exercise A: so or because? Exercise B: rewrite with the other word. Exercise C: put the story in order. Seven minutes."*
2. Pairs check. Check as a class.

### Stage 4: Freer practice (18 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To tell and write a story using the linking words. **Interaction:** Pairs, then groups, then whole class

1. **Picture story (8 minutes).** Give out Handout 3. Pairs look at six pictures of Malsha's day at the beach and tell the story using sequencers, *so* and *because*.
   - ICQs: *"How many sequencing words must you use?"* (At least four.)
2. **My terrible day (6 minutes).** Students tell a partner about a real or imaginary bad day with at least two *so* and two *because*.
3. **Write (4 minutes).** Students write their story in five or six sentences. Two read theirs aloud.
4. Monitor and note errors.

### Stage 5: Feedback and wrap-up (7 minutes)

**Stage aim:** To give feedback and consolidate. **Interaction:** Whole class

1. Board four sentences from your notes (two with errors). Students correct them.
2. Quick chain: one student says a sentence with *so*; the next continues with *because*.
3. Set the homework.

### Stage 6: Homework

**Set at the end of the lesson.**

Write the story of your best or worst day (100 words). Use *First, Then, After that, Finally*, and *so* and *because* at least twice each.

## Differentiation

**Support**

- Give weaker students the arrow diagram.
- In the picture story, give them the verbs for each picture.

**Stretch**

- Stronger students use *before* and *after* + -ing or clause: *After I had breakfast, I ...*
- They use *as a result* and *that's why*.

## Assessment and success criteria

- 10 out of 12 or better on Handout 2, Exercises A and B.
- In their stories, students use *so* for results and *because* for reasons.
- Students use at least four different sequencing words.

## Teacher's reflection

- Did the arrow diagram help with *so* and *because*?
- Were the stories more organised than in Stage 1?

# Handout 1: Kavindu's Terrible Day

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

Last Monday was a terrible day for Kavindu.

First, his alarm didn't ring, so he woke up late. Then he couldn't find his shoes because his little sister hid them! After that, he ran to the bus stop, but the bus left without him, so he took a tuk-tuk.

At school, the teacher was angry because Kavindu was 30 minutes late. Next, he had a maths test, and he didn't do well because he was very tired.

Finally, he got home and his mother said, "Surprise! It's pizza for dinner!" So the day ended well after all.

**Answer the questions.**

1. Why did Kavindu wake up late? ______________________________
2. Why couldn't he find his shoes? ______________________________
3. How did he get to school? ______________________________
4. Why didn't he do well in the test? ______________________________

# Handout 2: Practice

Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________

**A. Write so or because.**

1. It was raining, ______ we stayed at home.
2. I went to bed early ______ I was tired.
3. The shop was closed, ______ I didn't buy any bread.
4. She was happy ______ she passed the exam.
5. I was hungry, ______ I made a sandwich.
6. We took a taxi ______ we were late.

**B. Rewrite the sentences. Use the other word.**

1. I didn't go to school because I was ill. → I was ill, so ______________________________
2. It was hot, so we went swimming. → We went swimming because ______________________________
3. He missed the train because he got up late. → ______________________________
4. The film was boring, so I fell asleep. → ______________________________
5. I called my friend because I needed help. → ______________________________
6. There was no milk, so I drank black tea. → ______________________________

**C. Put the story in order (1–4).**

___ Then I put the flour, sugar and eggs in a bowl. ___ Finally, we ate the cake with my family. ___ First, I went to the shop and bought eggs. ___ After that, I baked it for 40 minutes.

# Answer Key

**Handout 1**

1. Because his alarm didn't ring. · 2. Because his little sister hid them. · 3. By tuk-tuk. · 4. Because he was very tired.

**Handout 2, Exercise A**

1. so · 2. because · 3. so · 4. because · 5. so · 6. because

**Handout 2, Exercise B**

1. ... so I didn't go to school. · 2. ... because it was hot. · 3. He got up late, so he missed the train. · 4. I fell asleep because the film was boring. · 5. I needed help, so I called my friend. · 6. I drank black tea because there was no milk.

**Handout 2, Exercise C**

2 · 4 · 1 · 3

**Handout 3 (model)**

First, Malsha packed a bag, but she forgot her sun cream. Then she took the train to Unawatuna. Next, she swam for two hours, so her back got red and painful because she didn't have any sun cream. After that, a storm started, so she ran to a café. Finally, she met an old school friend there, so the day ended happily.

**Common errors to watch for**

| **Error** | **Correction** |
| :-- | :-- |
| I was hungry because I ate a sandwich. | I was hungry, so I ate a sandwich. |
| It rained so we stayed home. | It rained, so we stayed home. |
| and then ... and then ... and then | First, ... Then ... After that, ... Finally, ... |
| I was late. So because I missed the bus. | I was late because I missed the bus. |
