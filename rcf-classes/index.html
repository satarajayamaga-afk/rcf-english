About the Academy

RCF Online Academy is the teaching arm of RCF English. Courses are conducted online, which allows students anywhere in the country to attend rather than only those within reach of one town.

All courses are taught by Rohana Fernando, a graduate in English of the University of Peradeniya, holder of a Master of Arts in Linguistics from the University of Kelaniya and a TESOL qualification from the University of Peradeniya, and an Associate Member of the Colombo Association of Language Skills and Dramatic Art (CALSDA). More about our staff.

Course details

About the diplomas, stated plainly

  • The elocution diplomas are set, examined and awarded by CALSDA — the Colombo Association of Language Skills and Dramatic Art.
  • RCF Online Academy conducts preparatory courses for those examinations. It does not award the diplomas.
  • RCF Online Academy is not an official representative, agent or branch of CALSDA, and does not speak for it.
  • Entry requirements, syllabus, examination dates and CALSDA's own fees are matters for CALSDA, and candidates should confirm them with CALSDA directly.

Course details
The same method throughout

How the courses are taught

Deliberately gradual, because most students fail at written answers and at prepared speech through lack of preparation rather than lack of ability.

  1. Clear and systematic explanation

    One point at a time, in plain language, with the reason behind it and not only the rule.

  2. Guided practice before independent work

    Nothing is set cold. A model is studied, the structure identified, and support withdrawn step by step.

  3. Individual correction and feedback

    Written and spoken work is corrected individually, with one clear instruction rather than a page of red.

  4. Examination preparation where relevant

    Paper structure, timing, answer development and past-paper practice for those preparing for an examination.

  5. Regular revision

    Deliberate return to earlier work, spaced out, because that is what makes learning permanent.

  6. Confidence, and support for those in difficulty

    Extra help for students who are struggling, without singling them out in front of others.

Five steps

How to register

  1. Choose a course

    Look through the courses above. If you are not sure which suits you, say what you have already done and you will be advised.

  2. Contact RCF Online Academy on WhatsApp

    Use the registration page or any WhatsApp button. The message is written for you; you add your details and press send.

  3. Receive schedule, fee and availability information

    You will be told the current day, time and fee, and whether places are available.

  4. Confirm registration

    Registration is confirmed by the teacher after this exchange. It is not automatic, and nothing on this website registers you.

  5. Receive joining instructions

    Once your place is confirmed you are sent the joining details privately. These are never published on this website.
