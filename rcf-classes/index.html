About RCF Classes

R. C. Fernando currently conducts mainly online classes through RCF Classes. These classes allow him to share the knowledge and practical experience developed over his long academic and professional career with learners regardless of their location.

Online delivery enables RCF Classes to reach students beyond a single geographical area and provide access to specialised English and literature instruction across the country.

He is a graduate in English of the University of Peradeniya, holds a Master of Arts in Linguistics from the University of Kelaniya and a TESOL qualification from the University of Peradeniya, and is an Associate Member of the Colombo Association of Language Skills and Dramatic Art (CALSDA). His full background is here.

How the classes are taught

The teaching approach

The same approach that runs through the lessons on this site. It is deliberately gradual, because most learners fail at writing and at literature answers through lack of preparation rather than lack of ability.

  1. Clear and systematic explanation

    One point at a time, in plain language, with the reason behind the rule and not only the rule.

  2. Guided practice before independent work

    No task is set cold. A model is studied, the structure is identified, and support is withdrawn step by step.

  3. Practical use of language

    English used for real purposes — writing a letter someone would actually send, saying something you would actually say.

  4. Individual correction and feedback

    Written work is corrected individually, with one clear instruction rather than a page of red.

  5. Examination preparation where relevant

    Paper structure, timing, answer development and past-paper practice, for those preparing for O/L or A/L.

  6. Regular revision

    Deliberate return to earlier work, spaced out, because that is what makes learning permanent.

  7. Confidence, and support for those in difficulty

    Extra help for learners who are struggling, without singling them out in front of others.

Frequently asked questions

How do I join a class?

Choose a class, contact RCF Classes on WhatsApp, and you will be sent the schedule, fee and availability information. Registration is confirmed by the teacher; it is not automatic and nothing on this website enrols you.

Why are the days, times and fees not shown for every class?

Because they change, and publishing an out-of-date schedule helps nobody. Where a detail has not been settled, this site says so rather than inventing a figure. Ask on WhatsApp and you will be told what currently applies.

Are the classes online or in a classroom?

Classes are conducted mainly online, which allows students in any part of the country to attend.

What do I need in order to attend?

A device with an internet connection, a notebook and a pen. Spoken English classes also need a working microphone. Literature classes need the prescribed texts.

Are classes in English only?

Classes are conducted in English, with explanation in simpler language where a learner needs it. Nobody is expected to understand everything immediately.

Can I join in the middle of a course?

It depends on the course and how far it has progressed. Ask on WhatsApp and you will be told honestly whether joining now would work for you.

Is there individual attention in a group class?

Written work is corrected individually in both group and individual classes. Learners who need more support than a group allows are usually better suited to individual classes.

Do you teach students who are very weak in English?

Yes. Support for learners experiencing difficulty is a particular emphasis of these classes, and of this whole platform.

Will I be sent a meeting link?

Joining instructions are sent privately once your place is confirmed. Meeting links and passwords are never published on this website.
