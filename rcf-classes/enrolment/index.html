RCF Classes

Enrolment

Enrolment is a conversation, not a form. Nothing on this website registers you for anything.

  1. Home
  2. RCF Classes
  3. Enrolment

The five steps

  1. Choose a class

    Look through the available courses. If you are unsure which suits you, say what you need — your grade, your examination, or what you find difficult — and you will be advised.

  2. Contact RCF Classes through WhatsApp

    Use one of the buttons on this page. The message is written for you. Add your own details before sending it.

  3. Receive schedule, fee and availability information

    You will be told the current day and time, the fee, and whether places are available. This information is given directly rather than published, because it changes.

  4. Confirm registration

    If the class suits you, confirm. Registration is confirmed by the teacher. It is not automatic, and no place is reserved by visiting this website.

  5. Receive joining instructions

    Once your place is confirmed, joining details are sent to you privately. They are never published on this website.

What you will be asked

So that you can have the answers ready, and get a useful reply the first time.

  • Which class you are interested in.
  • Your grade or level — or, for General English, roughly what you can already do.
  • Whether you are preparing for an examination, and in which year.
  • Whether you would prefer individual or group classes.
  • Which days and times would be possible for you.
  • Anything you find particularly difficult, so the teaching can take account of it.

Questions about enrolling

Can I register on this website?

No. This website has no server and no registration system. Enrolment is arranged directly with the teacher on WhatsApp, and a place is confirmed only by the teacher.

Is any payment taken on this website?

No. RCF English does not take payments of any kind, and no payment details should ever be entered anywhere on this site. Fee arrangements are discussed directly.

How soon will I get a reply?

Messages are answered as soon as possible. If you have not heard within a couple of days, please send a reminder — messages are occasionally missed.

What if the class times do not suit me?

Say so. Times are sometimes arranged around the students who want a particular class, especially for individual classes.

Can I try a class before deciding?

Ask. Whether this is possible depends on the course and where it has reached, and you will be told honestly.

Can my parent make the enquiry for me?

Of course. Please say who the student is, their grade, and what they need.

Please note

  • Nothing on this website constitutes an offer of a place. Class information here is descriptive.
  • Do not send personal documents, identity numbers or payment details in an enquiry message.
  • Joining links and passwords are never published here and are sent privately after confirmation.
