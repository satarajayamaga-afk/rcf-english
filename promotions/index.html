Packages

Advertise Your Class or Course

Three options are available. Rates are not published here because they are reviewed from time to time, so please ask for the current figure before you plan.

  • Weekly Listing

    Displayed for 7 days

    Contact us for current rates

    A standard listing in the main directory for one week. Suitable for a short course, a workshop or a single intake.

  • Monthly Listing

    Displayed for 30 days

    Contact us for current rates

    A standard listing for a full month. Suitable for regular weekly classes and term-long courses.

  • Featured Promotion

    Displayed for the agreed period

    Contact us for current rates

    A more prominent position at the top of the directory, with a Featured label, for the period agreed with you.

Five steps

How an advertisement is published

  1. You send the details

    Send your class information and your poster using the enquiry below.

  2. We review it

    We check that the advertisement is educational, clear and accurate.

  3. The fee is paid

    The advertising fee is paid in advance of publication.

  4. It is published

    Your listing appears for the period you have paid for.

  5. It ends

    At the end of the period the listing is removed or marked expired.

How this form works

Sending your poster

The form above cannot carry an image, because this website has no server to receive one. Send your poster as a picture in the same WhatsApp conversation once the message opens, or attach it to an email to us.
