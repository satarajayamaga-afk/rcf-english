Grade 10 English

Grade 10 completes the passive voice. Having met it in the simple tenses at Grade 8 and the past and future at Grade 9, the syllabus now works through every perfect and continuous form. O/L preparation begins here in earnest.

  1. Home
  2. Grade 6-11 Resources
  3. Grade 10 English

Grade 10 grammar practice

The passive voice in perfect and continuous forms, If Type 3, and discourse markers. These follow competency levels 6.1, 6.10 and 3.7.

Listening

Grade 10 listening practice

Five complete listening tests: a presentation to be summarised in notes, a three-way discussion, a news bulletin, a talk built on signal words, and a staff room conversation.

Choose Practice to replay freely, or Formal test for examination conditions. The transcript appears only after you submit. These follow competency levels 3.4, 3.6, 3.7 and 3.8.
