For everyday life

General English

English for the situations you actually meet: at the counter, on the telephone, at an interview, in an email, with a stranger asking for directions. No examination, no marks — just English you can use.

Start with practical grammarCheck your levelInteractive practice
  1. Home
  2. General English

Who this section is for

For adults who studied English at school and have forgotten most of it. For school leavers who need English for work. For people who understand English perfectly well but cannot bring themselves to speak it. For anyone who has been told, once too often, that their English is not good enough.

Nothing here is graded, marked or examined. You are not competing with anybody, and the only measure that matters is whether you can do something today that you could not do last month.

The one thing that matters most

The largest single obstacle for most adult learners is not grammar. It is the fear of making a mistake in front of someone.

Nobody has ever learnt a language without getting it wrong first, and a listener who understands you has understood you, whatever the grammar was doing. Speak imperfectly today and you will speak well next year; say nothing today and next year you will still be silent.

About levels on this site

Some material here is labelled A1, A2, B1 or B2 to give you a rough idea of difficulty: A1 and A2 for beginners and elementary learners, B1 and B2 for intermediate and upper-intermediate.

These labels are a guide only. RCF English does not claim that its material is formally aligned to the Common European Framework of Reference, and no test on this site provides a CEFR certificate or an official level.
