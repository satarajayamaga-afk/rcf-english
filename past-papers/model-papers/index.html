Model Papers

Papers written in the style of the examination. Useful when you have exhausted the real papers, or want practice on a specific format.

  1. Home
  2. Past Papers
  3. Model Papers

What a model paper is

A model paper is written to resemble the examination in structure, in the kinds of question asked and in the time it takes. It is not an official paper and is not set by any examination authority.

Model papers published here are written by RCF English, or are published with the permission of whoever wrote them. The source is shown against each one.
