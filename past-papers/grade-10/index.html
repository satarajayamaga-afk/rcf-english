Past Papers

Grade 10 Papers

Grade 10 English Language and English Literature term test papers. Every paper here has been opened and checked: the grade, subject and term match, the source printed on the paper has been recorded, and no paper carrying student information is listed.

How to use past papers wellCopyright policy
  1. Home
  2. Past Papers
  3. Grade 10 Papers

What this collection actually contains

The papers are heavily weighted to 2019. That is the shape of the collection, not a gap in the filtering. Most of the papers gathered so far come from 2018 and 2019, and there are no 2023 papers at all. A few recent papers are included, and they are generally the clearest scans.

The files are not stored on this website. Each paper opens in Google Drive. View the paper reads it in your browser; Download PDF saves it. Nothing is copied onto this site.

More papers are still being checked. A paper is added only after somebody has opened the file and confirmed what is in it, so this list grows slowly and will keep growing.
